### [1] [Refining and Aligning Clinical Reasoning with Health Guidelines via Formal Constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32247)
*Xiaoyang Fan,Yufan Cai,Zhe Hou,Jin Song Dong*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 本文提出可解释框架VerClin，通过将健康指南转化为可执行知识、四值确定性推理器、多专科适用性筛选和带形式约束的指导合成流程，实现和健康指南严格对齐的临床推理，能够识别触发信号较弱的高风险临床建议，在多个医疗评测基准上性能超过直接生成和Top-5 RAG基线。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有基于大模型的临床推理方案在处理不完整、存在冲突的患者证据时，很难同时兼顾低概率但高风险的医疗关切，并且难以严格对齐官方健康指南要求，容易出现生成内容不合规、漏检高风险临床建议的问题。

Method: 设计VerClin可解释框架：1. 将健康指南中的条件、排除项、推荐内容、优先级提取为可执行知识，将患者对话表征为限定范围的病例；2. 采用确定性四值推理器生成可追溯的指南匹配结果，在保留不确定性的同时强制执行指南排除规则；3. 通过多个专科代理筛选匹配指南的病例适用性，再经保留语句的形式约束通道结合专科推荐合成合规指导，最终按照优先级与触发强度排名之和升序排列输出推荐内容，优先展示触发信号弱的高风险指南条目。

Result: 在HealthBench Hard和HealthBench Professional数据集上，VerClin性能优于直接生成方法和Top-5 RAG方法；在LLMEval-Med子集上，性能超过直接生成方法，和Top-5 RAG持平；组件消融实验分别验证了语义闭合、示例引导优化、专科筛选、约束条件输出各模块的增益效果。

Conclusion: 基于形式约束的VerClin框架可以有效将临床推理和健康指南对齐，推理过程全程可追溯可解释，同时能够有效覆盖低触发强度的高风险临床指导，在主流医疗评测基准上取得优于现有主流基线的表现。

Abstract: Clinical reasoning from incomplete or conflicting patient evidence must account for low-probability but high-risk concerns while remaining grounded in health guidelines. We introduce VerClin, an explainable framework that extracts guideline conditions, exclusions, recommendations, and priorities into executable knowledge and represents patient conversations as scoped cases. A deterministic four-valued reasoner produces traceable guideline matches while preserving uncertainty and enforcing exclusions. Multiple specialty agents then screen matched guidelines for case applicability, and a consultation model synthesizes accepted guidance through specialty recommendations and a sentence-preserving formal-constraint channel. We present accepted guidance in ascending order of the sum of its priority and trigger-strength ranks, which can surface high-risk guidance with weaker triggers. VerClin outperforms direct generation and Top-5 RAG on HealthBench Hard and HealthBench Professional. On the LLMEval-Med subset, it outperforms direct generation and ties Top-5 RAG. Component analyses further examine semantic closure, example-guided refinement, specialty screening, and constraint-conditioned delivery.

### [2] [Measurement Artifacts in Trustworthiness Evaluations of Quantized Small Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32848)
*Tanzeel Abbas,Tehseen Zia,David Windridge*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 该论文针对参数量低于20亿的量化小语言模型的可信度评估问题，系统性研究了评估流程中存在的测量伪影现象，证实评估工具与量化操作的交互误差会错误生成甚至反向呈现量化对模型可信度的影响，通过多模型对照实验量化了四类常见测量选择引入的偏差，公开了配套标注数据集并给出压缩小模型可信度评估的规范建议。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有针对量化语言模型的可信度评估普遍默认基准测试的得分变化完全由量化操作本身导致，没有考虑评估测量环节引入的、会和量化交互的工具误差，这类误差会制造虚假的量化效果甚至反转真实结论，导致大量已发表的量化影响研究结论不可靠，因此需要对这类测量伪影的影响进行系统性量化与校正。

Method: 研究在8个20亿参数以下的指令微调小模型上，分别部署float16、LLM.int8()、NF4三种精度，在完全一致的测试样本上，系统评估四类测量选择和量化的交互误差：对照双人工标注结果校正关键词检测器的非拒绝率统计偏差、对比完形填空评分与字母选项多选评分的量化效果估计差异、测试批次组成和填充操作对不同量化策略输出的影响、拆解已报道的校准损失的真实来源。

Result: 1. 校正后对比人工标注结果，4位量化下有害合规率的上升幅度仅为未校正关键词检测器测得的非拒绝率上升幅度的1/2到2/3；2. 完形填空评分和字母多选评分对量化效果的估计相关性仅为0.10，远低于约0.77的理论上限，二者分歧远大于其固有测量噪声的允许范围；3. LLM.int8()的输出高度依赖同批次的其他测试样本，仅调整填充操作就会改变其输出，该现象由激活值8位取整导致，而NF4量化具备批次不变性；4. 此前公开报道的两个最大的量化校准损失实际上来源于响应偏置偏移，而非量化操作本身带来的性能变化。

Conclusion: 量化小语言模型的可信度评估流程中广泛存在测量工具与量化交互产生的伪影，这类伪影会严重扭曲甚至完全反转量化对模型可信度影响的测量结论，研究公开了所有样本级预测结果、选项得分和人工标注数据集，同时给出了压缩小模型可信度评估的可落地最佳实践建议。

Abstract: Evaluations of quantized language models attribute the change in a benchmark score to quantization. We write the measured change as the true change plus a term contributed by the instrument. Instrument error that is the same at every precision can shrink a paired comparison but cannot create or reverse an effect. Error that interacts with quantization can do both, and it is reported as an effect of quantization. We estimate this interaction for four measurement choices in 8 instruction-tuned models below two billion parameters, at float16, LLM.int8() and NF4, on identical items. Measured against labels from two human annotators, the 4-bit rise in harmful compliance is about half to two-thirds of the rise in non-refusal that a corrected key word detector records. Cloze and lettered multiple-choice scoring disagree about the effect of quantization far more than their noise allows: their estimates correlate at \r{ho} = 0.10, against a ceiling of about 0.77. LLM.int8() answers and generations depend on which items share a batch, and padding alone changes its answers. Rounding activations to 8 bits is enough to produce this dependence. NF4 is batch-invariant. The two largest reported calibration losses are response-bias shifts. We release item-level predictions, option scores and human annotations, and give recommendations for evaluating compressed small models.

### [3] [Speculative Decoding for Semantic-ID-Based Generative Recommendations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32855)
*Harshal A. Chaudhari*

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: 本文针对基于语义ID(Semantic-ID, SID)的生成式推荐系统现有约束束搜索推理效率低、无法保证输出是否为精确Top-K的问题，提出SIDECAR推测解码框架，通过近似草稿候选生成、树注意力单次打分和分支定界回退机制，在大幅减少模型调用次数的同时可认证输出是否为精确Top-K，还能给出输出和精确结果的偏差边界，支持质量感知的动态提前终止。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当前工业级部署的基于Semantic-ID的生成推荐系统普遍使用约束束搜索检索候选物品，存在两个核心缺陷：1）每个请求必须执行L次顺序模型调用，推理开销固定且偏高；2）当SID生成器锐度下降时推荐精度会劣化，且无法保证输出结果和全局精确Top-K的接近程度，生产系统无法感知单个请求的检索质量是否退化。

Method: 提出专为SID生成推荐场景设计的SIDECAR推测解码方案：1）首先生成近似草稿候选，通过树注意力前向传播在单次模型调用内完成全部草稿的打分，内存允许的情况下为所有未探索的SID前缀的概率给出严格上界；2）如果草稿已经包含全局精确Top-K集合，则直接完成结果认证，否则通过分支定界策略仅扩展仍有可能改变最终输出的前缀trie分支，最多L次模型调用即可完成结果认证；3）设定单调用处理M个token、总允许Y次调用的资源约束，当Y=L时总资源等价于宽度为M的传统束搜索，可输出所有排名下结果与精确集的偏差上界1+δ。

Result: SIDECAR可以在远低于传统束搜索的模型调用次数或内存开销下返回精确的Top-K推荐结果；即使分配的计算预算不足以恢复精确Top-K，SIDECAR也可以明确报告输出结果与精确集的偏差因子，允许服务系统在达到预设质量目标或延迟上限时就提前终止推理。

Conclusion: 针对基于Semantic-ID的生成推荐场景设计的SIDECAR推测解码方案，突破了传统束搜索固定高开销、无结果质量可认证性的局限，能够以更低的推理成本提供可验证的精确Top-K结果，且支持质量感知的动态提前终止，完全适配大规模生产部署的实际需求。

Abstract: Generative recommender systems with Semantic IDs (SIDs) represent each item as a short sequence of $L$ discrete tokens and use a sequence model to predict the next item's tokens from a user's history. Industry-scale deployments typically use these models to retrieve multiple candidates per request with constrained beam search. However, this incurs a fixed cost of $L$ sequential model calls per request, and accuracy degrades as the SID generator becomes less sharp, without a guarantee on how close the output is to the exact top-$K$. Production systems therefore cannot detect degraded retrieval for individual requests.
  We propose SIDECAR, which uses approximate draft candidates and a tree-attention pass to score the entire draft in a single model call, memory permitting. This pass bounds the probability of every unexplored SID prefix and certifies the exact top-$K$ if the draft contains them. Otherwise, a branch-and-bound fallback expands only the parts of the trie that can still change the output, reaching certification within at most $L$ calls for any draft. Moreover, under a budget of $M$ tokens per call and $Y$ calls, which for $Y = L$ matches the resources of a beam search of width $M$, SIDECAR announces a factor $1 + δ$ within which its list will be exact at every rank. Depending on model sharpness, SIDECAR returns the exact top-$K$ using a fraction of beam search's calls or memory. Even when the budget is too small to recover the exact top-$K$, SIDECAR, unlike beam search, reports the announced factor, allowing the serving system to stop early once a quality target or latency limit is reached.

### [4] [Towards Realistic Evaluation of Uncertainty Quantification for LLM-Based Recommender Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33036)
*Hao Wu,Sipeng Chen,Weikai Li,Tianyi Chen,Qiancheng Yang,Hang Zhou,Yizhou Sun,Wei Wang,Xiao Luo*

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: 该工作针对现有基于LLM的推荐系统中不同不确定性量化（UQ）方法难以公平对比的痛点，提出固定排序基准，在4个推荐数据集、2种LLM骨干上对8类异构UQ信号作为记录级监测器开展统一对照评估，得到了3项关键观察结果，为LLM推荐系统UQ的现实化标准化评估提供了基准框架和实证结论。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM驱动的推荐排序器没有标准物品分类头，其输出分数依赖选项标签、提示词、生成流程以及可获取的用户物品证据，现有UQ方法的信号来源不同、应用于推荐流程的不同阶段，部分方法仅评估已生成排序、部分方法直接使用不确定性修改排序结果，导致不同UQ方法之间难以直接公平比较，缺乏统一的现实评估框架。

Method: 提出固定排序评估基准，在共享输出约束下将8种异构UQ信号作为记录级监测器进行统一评估，遵循共享的固定排序协议在4个推荐数据集、2种LLM骨干上开展对照实验，从排序失败检测、跨推荐条件鲁棒性、受控证据变化响应三个维度分析不同UQ方法的特性差异。

Result: 得到三项核心实证发现：1）即使针对完全相同的参考实例，UQ方法的性能排序也会随所使用的LLM骨干变化发生改变；2）与排序对齐的Top10Mass在多数评估场景下取得了最优的标量Error@10检测性能，具备优秀的截断对齐监测能力，但该优势在面向流行度长尾目标时会明显收窄；3）通用标量UQ信号很难识别配对控制场景下哪一侧的截断NDCG@10差异更大，而扰动秩不稳定性、简单列表距离这类源特定UQ信号的配对控制一致性表现更强，对两类受控证据源的灵敏度更高。

Conclusion: 研究指出应当将排序失败检测、跨推荐条件鲁棒性、对受控证据变化的响应作为UQ方法的三类独立属性分别进行评估，该工作提出的基准框架和实证观察为LLM推荐系统不确定性量化的现实化、标准化评估提供了明确的指引。

Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) provide a flexible interface for recommendation systems. However, their issued rankings require reliable instance-level uncertainty monitoring. In this scenario, comparing uncertainty quantification (UQ) methods becomes challenging because LLM rankers lack a standard item-classification head. Their scores may depend on option labels, prompts, generation procedures, and the available user and item evidence. Existing methods derive uncertainty from different sources and apply it at different stages of the recommendation process. Some methods evaluate an issued ranking, whereas others use uncertainty to change the ranking itself, making direct comparison difficult. Here we introduce a fixed-ranking benchmark which evaluates eight heterogeneous UQ signals as record-level monitors under a shared output contract. We evaluate these signals across four recommendation datasets and two LLM backbones under a shared fixed-ranking protocol. From the results, we draw three main observations. First, UQ method ordering changes across backbones even on the same reference instances. Second, ranking-aligned Top10Mass provides the strongest scalar Error@10 detection in several evaluation conditions, reflecting effective cutoff-aligned monitoring, while its advantage narrows for popularity-tail targets. Third, scalar UQ signals are weak at identifying which control has the larger clipped NDCG@10 difference, whereas source-specific signals, including Perturbation Rank Instability and simple listwise distances, provide stronger paired-control agreement, reflecting their sensitivity to the two controlled evidence sources. These findings motivate us to evaluate ranking-failure detection, robustness across recommendation conditions, and responses to controlled evidence changes as distinct properties of UQ.
