### [1] [From Data Room to Report: End-to-End AI Support for M&A Legal Due Diligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34120)
*Johannes R. Hübers,Daniel Uedelhoven,Manuela Bergau,Markus Beyer,Lars Weber,Tobias Riethmüller,Arkadiusz Rzepka,Tobias Schuh,Maximilian Glaß,Omer Faruk Aydin,Jens Henneberg,Thiago Bell Felix de Oliveira,Lars Hillebrand,Rafet Sifa*

Main category: cs.CL

TL;DR: 本文提出了名为LEXI的端到端大语言模型辅助系统，覆盖并购交易法律尽调从异构文档处理到尽调报告预生成的完整流程，在保留律师对核心法律判断最终控制权的前提下，自动完成文档分类、关联目标实体、信息提取带溯源标记、报告内容草稿生成等事务性工作，初步评估验证了方案的可行性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统并购法律尽调需要律师手动处理虚拟数据室内海量异构文档、筛选交易相关信息并整理输出尽调报告，人力成本高、流程效率低，此前缺乏覆盖全工作流的可控AI支撑方案，同时需要避免AI替代律师做出专业法律决策，保留律师的核心判断权责。

Method: 设计实现了LEXI（Legal Extraction and Intelligence）原型系统，基于大语言模型，通过标准化结构化输出衔接全流程各环节：1. 按照可配置的尽调分类体系完成文档分类，为文档分配对应关联的目标实体；2. 提取预定义的交易相关信息，同时保留对应的支撑原文段落、页码级溯源信息；3. 基于已校验的提取结果自动预生成报告草稿内容。所有AI生成的中间结果均支持律师审核校正，法律评估、风险评级、最终报告措辞等核心法律权责完全由律师保留。

Result: 针对系统的文档分类、信息提取核心组件开展了初步评估，获取的实验结果证明该端到端AI辅助法律尽调的技术路线具备可行性，系统可顺畅打通从文档接入到报告预生成的全工作流。

Conclusion: LEXI验证了将大语言模型集成到并购法律尽调完整工作流的可行路径，可以有效降低律师的低价值事务性工作负担，同时完全保留律师对实质性法律判断的控制权，为AI赋能专业法律服务提供了可落地的参考框架。

Abstract: Legal due diligence in mergers and acquisitions requires lawyers to organize heterogeneous virtual data room documents, extract transaction-relevant information and consolidate reviewed findings into a due diligence report. We present LEXI (Legal Extraction and Intelligence), a work-in-progress system that demonstrates how large language models can support this process from document ingestion to report preparation. LEXI classifies documents under a configurable due diligence taxonomy, assigns relevant target entities, extracts predefined information with supporting passages and page-level provenance and prepares report content from reviewed findings. Structured outputs connect these stages and allow model-generated results to be passed through the workflow in a controlled form. Lawyers review and correct classifications and extractions and retain responsibility for legal assessments, recommendations, risk ratings and final report wording. Initial evaluations of the classification and extraction components provide preliminary evidence that the approach is feasible. LEXI illustrates how AI can be integrated across the legal due diligence workflow while preserving lawyer control over substantive legal judgment.

### [2] [Music Similarity Estimation from Jukebox Embeddings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34346)
*Johannes Grafinger,Gerhard Widmer,Jan Schlüter*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 本文提出了基于生成式音乐模型Jukebox输出嵌入的音乐相似度估计新方法JukeSim，在多数据集上验证其性能与现有主流学习型方法持平，且二者均显著优于此前最优的人工设计方法，证明了利用生成式预训练音乐嵌入完成该任务的可行性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 内容驱动的音乐相似度估计是无用户交互数据的冷启动音乐推荐的核心支撑技术，但此前该领域尚未将基于深度学习的最新方案与传统人工设计特征驱动的方法开展系统性对比，同时预训练生成音乐模型的嵌入在音乐相似度任务上的有效性尚未得到验证。

Method: 提出JukeSim音乐相似度估计方案，直接复用生成式音乐模型Jukebox预训练输出的音频嵌入，计算不同音乐录音之间的相似度，无需额外专门训练相似度度量模型。

Result: 在多个测试数据集上的实验结果显示，JukeSim的表现与现有成熟的学习型音乐相似度估计方法性能相当，两类方法均大幅超越此前最优的人工设计相似度度量方法；仅JukeSim的计算开销高于其他传统相似度度量方案。

Conclusion: 该研究验证了将生成式音乐预训练模型的嵌入用于音乐相似度估计任务的可行性，为后续相关研究提供了新的方向，可支撑冷启动音乐推荐等实际场景的需求。

Abstract: Content-based music similarity estimation aims to provide a measure of similarity between music audio recordings, disregarding any associated metadata. This has potential applications in e.g. cold-start music recommendation scenarios, where there is no user interaction data. In this work, we propose JukeSim, a new approach to estimating musical similarity based on embeddings created by Jukebox, a generative music model. In evaluations on multiple datasets, we find that JukeSim performs on par with an existing learning-based approach, and both outperform the best hand-designed approaches, which had never been compared to recent work before. Even though the required compute for JukeSim is higher than for other measures, we demonstrate the viability of utilizing generative embeddings for this task, encouraging further research.

### [3] [Routeability Before Routing: Routeability Audit Protocol (RAP) and RouteabilityBench for Audited LLM Model Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34825)
*Yihang Lu,Denica Kjorvezir,Ana Gjorgjevikj,Carola Doerr,Tome Eftimov*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 本文提出了以可路由性为核心的前置审核机制RAP和可运行验证包RouteabilityBench，用于在大语言模型路由方法的性能对比被解读为通用结论前，先完成对基准数据集和候选模型组合的可路由性校验，实验发现多数通用路由增强策略无法稳定超过候选集中的单最佳模型，提出路由相关工作必须配套公开可路由性诊断等必要信息以保证结论可信度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有LLM路由研究直接在粗粒度评分的公开基准上开展路由方案性能对比，但这类基准普遍存在多模型并列拿到最高分、全局最优单模型几乎占满性能上限的问题，导致路由方案的性能提升没有实际价值，行业缺乏在路由性能验证前对基准-模型组合可路由性的前置校验流程。

Method: 设计了事后执行的路由优先审核协议RAP，配套可运行的声明验证包RouteabilityBench，该流程在基准-候选模型的得分矩阵生成之后、路由器对比结论输出之前执行，覆盖对更大嵌入集、降维、选择性弃权、聚类、高性能回归器等所有常见通用路由增强技术的系统性校验。

Result: 在4个主流LLM基准、总计66个大语言模型的实验中验证，绝大多数通用路由增强策略无法稳定取得超出候选集中单最佳模型的性能收益；仅在特定模型组合的Omni-MATH场景下路由呈现明确正向效果，GPQA、MMLU-Pro、IFEval三个基准分别展现了路由任务难度极高、预测器结果敏感等不同的低可路由性特征。

Conclusion: LLM路由相关研究在报告路由性能增益时，必须同步披露可路由性诊断结果、并列最优模型分析、候选模型组合构建细节、经过家族校正的统计检验结果，才能保障结论的可靠性与可推广性。

Abstract: LLM routing aims to choose, for each prompt, which model in a portfolio is most likely to answer correctly. This only makes sense when the benchmark contains enough prompt-specific variation for different models to be best on different inputs. On coarse-scored LLM benchmarks, however, many models often tie for the best score, and the single best model may already leave little room for improvement. We therefore propose a routeability-first audit, instantiated as RAP and RouteabilityBench, a runnable claim-verification package. The audit is retrospective: it runs once the benchmark-portfolio score matrix exists and before router comparisons are interpreted as general evidence, rather than certifying a portfolio that has not yet been evaluated. Across four benchmarks and 66 LLMs, generic routing enhancements such as larger embedding sets, dimensionality reduction, selective abstention, clustering, and stronger regressors do not consistently outperform the single best model. The clearest positive evidence is concentrated in portfolio-dependent Omni-MATH settings, while GPQA, MMLU-Pro, and IFEval show different ways in which routing can remain hard or predictor-sensitive. Our practical recommendation is that routing gains should be reported together with routeability diagnostics, tied-best analysis, portfolio construction details, and family-corrected statistical tests.

### [4] [Robust Recourse under Causal Graph Ambiguity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34723)
*Muyan Anna Li,Juliette Sinnott,Mohammad Kohandel,Amir-Hossein Karimi*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 针对现有因果算法追索方法忽略因果图歧义的缺陷，本文提出两种鲁棒追索方法，可在存在多组拟合观测数据的候选因果图场景下输出低代价的有效干预动作，并通过实验验证了追索覆盖率与动作真实有效性的可权衡特性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有因果算法追索方法普遍预设唯一确定的因果图来建模干预对特征的影响，仅考虑因果机制的不确定性，但观测数据通常可拟合出多个效果预测差异很大的因果图，忽略这类因果图歧义会导致推荐的追索动作在真实系统中失效，无法满足实际场景需求。

Method: 提出两类新的追索范式：1）最坏情况追索，搜索在所有候选可行因果图下都能生效的最低成本动作；2）图加权追索，搜索在指定比例权重的候选图集合内生效的最低成本动作。为每个候选因果图拟合高斯过程结构方程，采用后验均值-标准差准则判断动作有效性，通过基于梯度的搜索求解最小成本动作，并在独立的全新后验样本上校验动作的有效性。

Result: 在合成和半合成基准实验中发现，放松要求动作在所有候选图下都生效的严格鲁棒性约束，可提升追索的实例覆盖率、降低动作的执行成本；在20节点GP基准上的测试结果显示，1000个测试实例中可获得追索的实例数从341提升至436，但真实生成模型下动作成功翻转决策的数量从251降至184，明确呈现了追索覆盖率和真实动作有效性之间的权衡关系。

Conclusion: 显式建模因果图歧义的鲁棒追索方法，能够在系统因果机制未被完全探明的场景下，为决策者推荐出有效性更有保障的追索动作，同时可以通过调整鲁棒性约束的严格程度，灵活平衡追索覆盖率和动作的真实有效性。

Abstract: Causal algorithmic recourse recommends actions to reverse unfavorable decisions by modeling how interventions change an individual's features. Existing methods typically assume one causal graph that describes the system and account for uncertainty in the causal mechanisms. However, there usually exist several graphs that fit the same data but can predict different effects for the same action. Certain actions may achieve recourse across multiple graphs, but as the set of potential graphs grows, a single best action may become infeasible.
  We propose Worst-Case Recourse and Graph-Weighted Recourse to find a low-cost action that succeeds across all candidate graphs or a specified fraction of graph weight, respectively. Assuming a set of plausible graphs, for each graph we fit Gaussian process structural equations and assess success using the posterior mean--standard-deviation criterion. We use gradient-based search to determine minimal cost actions and check the validity of the actions on fresh posterior samples. In synthetic and semi-synthetic benchmarks, relaxing robustness, the requirement that an action succeed under every candidate graph, can increase the number of valid recommendations for recourse and lower costs of actions for both methods. In the 20-node GP benchmark, relaxation increases the number of factual instances offered recourse (coverage) from 341 to 436 out of 1,000, while the number of true successes, actions that flip the decision under the generating model, falls from 251 to 184, illustrating the coverage--validity trade-off. Accounting for graph ambiguity can help decision-makers recommend actions that remain effective even when the causal mechanisms of the system are not fully known.

</details>
