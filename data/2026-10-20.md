### [1] [EASformer: Environment-Action-State Alignment for Industrial Generative Ranking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27691)
*Bohao Li,Dinglun Cao,Pengcheng Huang,Yingda Tang,Zhiting Zhao,Yi Cao,Jilei Fu,Miao Yan,Tianying Liu,Heng Chang,Bowen Du*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 本文针对工业生成式排序中序列和非序列特征表示语义不对称的问题，提出了环境-动作-状态解耦对齐的EASformer模型，离线测试和在线A/B测试均证明其效果优于现有部署基线。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现代工业排序系统普遍采用生成式推荐范式，用Transformer统一建模用户行为序列和非序列特征，但历史序列表示和候选侧非序列表示存在语义不对称：历史表示混合了环境上下文和观测动作语义，而候选非序列表示没有观测动作但包含更丰富补充上下文，直接让两类表示交互会导致语义不匹配的点积计算，限制有效的跨token交互，因此需要解决该语义不对称问题。

Method: 提出EASformer，一种环境-动作-状态解耦对齐的架构：1.构建历史token和非序列token共享的显式对齐环境上下文空间；2.从历史token中解耦出用户动作，从非序列token中解耦出候选特定上下文；3.基于对齐空间实现互补历史动作检索和状态条件动作预测；4.设计了完形填空式自监督目标，通过掩码历史动作复用候选预测路径进行训练。

Result: 在真实工业应用市场的离线基准上，AUC达到0.89411，相对已部署基线提升0.88%；在线A/B测试中，两个真实排序场景的eCPM分别相对提升1.26%和3.94%。

Conclusion: EASformer通过环境-动作-状态对齐解耦有效解决了生成式排序中语义不对称问题，在离线和在线任务中均取得显著效果提升，适用于工业排序场景。

Abstract: Modern industrial ranking systems increasingly adopt generative recommendation (GR) paradigms, in which user behavior sequences and non-sequential (NS) features are tokenized and jointly modeled by Transformer-style backbones. This unified formulation provides a scalable interface for modeling long histories and heterogeneous features. However, sequence and NS representations often carry asymmetric semantics: historical representations may combine environment context with observed-action semantics, whereas candidate-side NS representations contain no observed action but incorporate richer supplementary context, such as user-item interaction features and recent behavior lists. Direct interaction between such representations can make query-key dot products compare different semantic objects, limiting meaningful cross-token interaction. To fill this gap, we propose EASformer, an Environment-Action-State disentangled architecture for industrial app ranking. EASformer constructs an explicitly aligned context environment space shared by historical and non-sequence tokens, while disentangling user actions from historical token and candidate-specific context form non-sequence tokens. Based on this aligned space, EASformer enables complementary historical action retrieval and state-conditioned action prediction. Furthermore, the disentangled design supports a Cloze-style self-supervised objective by masking historical actions and reusing the candidate prediction pathway. On an offline benchmark from a real-world industrial app marketplace, EASformer achieves an AUC of 0.89411, yielding a 0.88% relative improvement over the deployed baseline. Online A/B tests further support this advantage, with relative eCPM lifts of 1.26% and 3.94% across two real-world ranking scenarios.

### [2] [Local Inference of LLMs on Long Contexts: A Data Systems Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28223)
*Mattias Dürrmeier,Alberto Lerner*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 本文从数据系统视角研究了单GPU上大语言模型长上下文本地推理的瓶颈问题，分析了不同模型在多代GPU上的运行表现，揭示了资源利用低效率问题，并为未来研究提出了建议。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当前长上下文大语言模型推理大多依赖多GPU的昂贵强大系统，本地推理虽具有无每token成本、无速率限制、隐私保护和用户可控的优势，但普遍观点认为本地LLM需要压缩才能在单GPU上运行，现有研究缺乏对除显存容量之外限制单用户长上下文模型服务的瓶颈分析。

Method: 不从单一模型单GPU角度表征，而是分析不同模型在多代GPU上的运行行为，从数据系统视角进行实验观察，梳理发现瓶颈和趋势。

Result: 证实了自注意力在长上下文推理中主导运行时间，更意外发现当前自注意力结构导致关键GPU资源利用率显著偏低，且最新GPU的资源利用情况往往最差，这些低效率本质上属于数据管理问题，涵盖KV缓存分页、缓存利用率提升和GPU同步等方面。

Conclusion: 长上下文本地LLM推理存在源于数据管理的GPU资源利用低效率问题，尤其是最新GPU上问题更突出，本文将该问题呈现给数据系统社区，并为未来相关工作提出了建议。

Abstract: Large language models can solve complex problems across a variety of subjects, such as mathematics and coding. For such complex analytical tasks, models with a long context perform better. Serving models with long context is currently mostly done on powerful systems with multiple GPUs, which are too expensive for most users. An alternative is local inference, or serving a model on a user's own machine, which removes per-token costs and rate limits, besides keeping sensitive data private and giving users full control over the models they run. The common understanding, however, is that local LLMs need compression (e.g., quantization) to run on a single GPU. In this paper, we set out to study the limitations that prevent serving long-context models for single users, besides the capacity issue. Rather than characterizing a single model on one GPU, we analyze how different models behave across several GPU generations. This lets us uncover not just individual bottlenecks but broader trends in where inefficiencies arise. Our analysis confirms that, as expected, self-attention dominates the runtime for long-context inference. More unexpectedly, our evaluation reveals that the current structure of self-attention leaves key GPU resources significantly underutilized, and the newest GPU is often the least well served. Many of these findings are fundamentally data management problems, from paging the KV cache to improving cache utilization and synchronization in the GPU, which is why we bring this study to the Data Systems community. To engage this community, we present our findings as a series of experimental observations and formulate recommendations for future work.

### [3] [When Health Assistants Agree Too Readily: A Benchmark of Time-Critical Self-Diagnosis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27518)
*Marilyn Zhang,Tianfeng Chen,Patricia M Pechter,Mark E. Whiting*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 本文提出了一个针对时间关键型自我诊断的配对基准SelfDx，测试大语言模型在健康咨询场景中是否能对需要谨慎处理的严重风险案例给出正确提醒，发现现有前沿模型普遍容易不当同意用户的自我诊断，进而提出有监督微调可同时提升模型正确提醒和正确同意的比例，给出了模型健康回答行为优化的建议。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 大量用户已经使用大语言模型咨询健康问题，模型过度认同用户自我诊断可能会延误紧急/重症医疗，现有研究缺乏对该风险场景下模型正确响应行为的基准测试和优化方法。

Method: 构建了包含100对样本的SelfDx基准，每对样本分别对应：存在严重疾病可能性需要提醒的谨慎案例，以及用户已排除严重风险可安全同意的同意案例；在27个前沿大模型上测试其响应行为，对比了提示工程和小样本有监督微调两种优化方法的效果。

Result: 27个前沿模型普遍存在不当同意用户自我诊断的问题，表现最好的当前一代模型在时间关键案例中也仅达到77.0%的正确提醒率；三种提示变体虽能提升正确提醒率，但会导致正确同意率下降9-35%，出现过度谨慎；在128个平衡样本上的有监督微调可以同时提升两项指标。

Conclusion: 现有大语言模型在时间关键型健康自我诊断场景中存在安全缺陷，后训练（有监督微调）可以在不导致全场景过度谨慎的前提下提升模型正确提醒率，配对评估方法可以同时验证模型两种正确响应行为。

Abstract: Every day, more than 40 million people ask ChatGPT a health question, such as whether laptop posture explains a stiff neck and days of pounding headache. Posture is plausible, but meningitis is a time-critical possibility. We introduce SelfDx, a paired benchmark for whether models have the appropriate response behavior, a form of epistemic independence, in health questions where misplaced agreement with the user's self-diagnosis could delay urgent or emergency care. Each of 100 pairs contrasts a caution case, where a serious alternative remains possible, with an agreement case, where the user supplies evidence that rules out the alternative and it is safe for the model to agree. We find across 27 frontier models that they tend to inappropriately agree with the user's self-diagnosis in cases that require caution. Even the best current-generation model reaches only a 77.0% appropriate caution rate on time-critical condition cases. To address this gap, we experiment with three prompt variants that increase appropriate caution but reduce appropriate agreement by 9-35%, indicating excessive caution. In contrast, supervised fine-tuning on 128 balanced examples improves both rates together across three runs. We recommend post-training to improve appropriate caution without making models cautious on every case, with paired evaluation to verify both response behaviors.

### [4] [SPAR: Stepwise Personalized Autoregressive Recommendation with Prefix-Balanced Semantic IDs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27898)
*Chunrui Zhang,Gaoyun Lin,Chang Zhou,Zheng Hu,Qiran Song,Shimin Cai,Tao Zhou*

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: This paper proposes SPAR, a stepwise personalized autoregressive generative recommendation framework based on semantic IDs, which addresses the problems of insufficient stepwise personalization and imbalanced branch loads in existing semantic ID-based generative recommendation, and achieves significant performance improvements on both offline large-scale production data and online A/B tests on Youku.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Existing semantic ID-based generative recommendation methods fail to sufficiently consider prefix-dependent user evidence and suffer from imbalanced historical interaction loads among candidate branches, which leads to irreversible branch selection errors and bias towards popular paths, degrading recommendation performance.

Method: Propose SPAR, a framework that jointly optimizes stepwise personalization and semantic ID construction: 1) PATH Decoder extracts prefix-relevant evidence from compressed long-term user memory, and combines it with recent behaviors, user profiles and event context to guide each token decision; 2) RQ-PIB module balances historical interaction loads among sibling branches under each parent prefix while preserving semantic structure of semantic IDs.

Result: On offline large-scale Youku production data, SPAR achieves 2.66% average relative HitRate improvement over the strongest baseline, increases target-prefix retention by 5.79 percentage points, and reduces traffic-weighted largest child-branch share by 32.84% across SID levels. In a 7-day online A/B test serving 10% of Youku's production traffic, it improves average watch time per user by 3.95% and play conversion rate by 3.38%.

Conclusion: SPAR achieves more reliable hierarchical decoding, balanced semantic ID branches, and improved user engagement for industrial generative recommendation, and is verified effective in real production environment.

Abstract: Semantic ID (SID)-based generative recommendation retrieves items through successive token-level decisions, making personalization at each decoding step essential. However, existing methods insufficiently account for prefix-dependent user evidence and imbalanced historical interaction loads among candidate branches. This can cause irreversible branch-selection errors and favor popular paths. We propose SPAR, a generative recommendation framework that jointly improves stepwise personalization and SID construction. Its PATH Decoder retrieves prefix-relevant evidence from compressed long-term user memories and combines it with recent behavior, user profiles, and event context to guide each token decision. Its RQ-PIB module balances historical interaction loads among sibling branches under each parent prefix while preserving semantic structure. Experiments on large-scale Youku production data show an average relative HitRate improvement of 2.66% over the strongest baseline. SPAR also increases target-prefix retention by 5.79 percentage points and reduces the traffic-weighted largest child-branch share by 32.84% across SID levels. In a 7-day online A/B test, SPAR serves 10% of Youku's production traffic and improves average watch time per user by 3.95% and play conversion rate by 3.38%. These results demonstrate more reliable hierarchical decoding, balanced SID branches, and improved user engagement in industrial recommendation.

### [5] [Evolving Semantic Indexes for Generative Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28568)
*Jinming Nian,Jyun-Yu Jiang,Henry Tsai,Yi Fang,Zhirong Liang*

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: 本文针对生成式检索中语义标识符（SID）组件无法端到端梯度优化、多为手工设计的问题，提出了基于大语言模型（LLM）驱动的进化程序搜索框架来优化生成式检索的语义索引组件，在多个公开数据集和电商数据集上取得了性能提升，验证了该方法的有效性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 生成式检索将搜索和推荐建模为离散语义标识符（SID）的自回归生成任务，SID设计直接决定生成式检索的性能，但SID相关的构造、冲突处理、解码、训练等离散相互依赖组件无法通过梯度进行端到端优化，目前大多依赖手工设计，因此需要自动化的优化方法改进生成式检索组件设计。

Method: 提出LLM驱动的进化程序搜索框架，使用本地部署的开放权重31B参数LLM对可执行的生成式检索组件进行变异，无需调用专有LLM API；采用1M参数的代理解码器替代重复训练1B参数的目标生成器，快速提供进化的反馈信号；利用代理模型提供的反馈引导进化，对SID构造和冲突处理等组件进行联合优化。

Result: 在NQ320K数据集上，代理模型和目标模型的Recall@100秩相关系数达0.90；联合进化SID构造和冲突处理后，NQ320K上Recall@1从55.8%提升到59.3%，Recall@100从85.8%提升到90.6%，在MSMARCO300K和多语言电商数据集上也获得稳定性能提升；组件研究表明，代理可靠性依赖于进化对象，语义索引的改进可可靠迁移，但生成器训练目标或全流水线的改进迁移性不佳。

Conclusion: LLM驱动的进化搜索是生成式检索设计的实用工具，只要针对选定搜索空间建立可靠的代理反馈，就能有效优化手工设计的生成式检索组件，提升检索性能。这也是首次将进化程序搜索应用到生成式检索领域。

Abstract: Generative retrieval (GR) casts search and recommendation as autoregressive generation of discrete semantic identifiers (SIDs). SID design largely determines GR quality, but its discrete, interdependent components (SID construction, collision handling, decoding, and training) cannot be optimized end-to-end with gradients and remain largely hand-designed. We introduce a framework that improves these components through LLM-driven evolutionary program search, to our knowledge the first application of this approach to GR. A locally served, open-weight 31B model mutates executable GR components, so no proprietary LLM API is used, and a 1M-parameter proxy decoder provides fast feedback in place of repeatedly training the 1B target generator. Across 21 SID programs on NQ320K, proxy and target Recall@100 show strong rank correlation (Spearman $ρ= 0.90$). Guided by this proxy, evolution improves the seed GR on NQ320K, MSMARCO300K, and a multilingual e-commerce dataset under a fixed training recipe. On NQ320K, jointly evolving SID construction and collision handling improves Recall@1 from 55.8% to 59.3% and Recall@100 from 85.8% to 90.6%. A component study shows that proxy reliability depends on what is evolved: improvements transfer reliably for the semantic index but not for the generator's training objective or the full pipeline. LLM-driven evolutionary search is thus a practical tool for GR design, provided proxy reliability is established for the chosen search space.

### [6] [Robustness Analysis of Pareto-based Joint Evaluation of Fairness and Relevance in Recommender Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27850)
*Theresia Veronika Rampisela,Maria Maistro,Tuukka Ruotsalo,Christina Lioma*

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于帕累托前沿的推荐系统公平性与相关性联合评估方法DPFR，实验验证该方法比现有联合评估方法更稳健，并给出了使用DPFR进行联合评估的指南。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有推荐系统公平性和相关性评估方法存在缺陷：分开评估会得到两个不同的模型排序，单指标联合评估往往对两个方面的评估不平衡，缺乏能同时稳健评估公平性和相关性的联合方法。

Method: 提出距离帕累托前沿法DPFR，基于现有度量得到的公平性和相关性分数构建帕累托前沿（代表两个方面不同的帕累托最优权衡），通过模型点到帕累托前沿的距离得到公平性和相关性的联合分数，该方法可兼容现有度量，具有模块化和直观性。使用真实世界数据集、人工推荐数据进行多组实验，对比DPFR与现有方法，分析其稳健性。

Conclusion: DPFR是一种模块化、直观的公平性和相关性联合评估方法，比现有方法更稳健，本文给出了使用DPFR进行联合评估的实践指南。

Abstract: Recommender Systems (RSs) can be evaluated for many aspects, two of them being recommendation relevance and fairness. These two aspects can be evaluated either in isolation, using a measure for each aspect; or jointly with a single`relevance-aware' fairness measure that quantifies fairness with respect to relevance. Yet, neither approach provides a simultaneous evaluation of both relevance and fairness: the first approach may result in two different rankings of models, each corresponding to an evaluation aspect; the second approach often provides an imbalanced evaluation of the two aspects. To address this gap, we propose Distance to Pareto Frontier (DPFR), a new approach to jointly evaluate RS fairness and relevance. DPFR is based on a Pareto frontier of relevance and fairness scores from existing measures. The frontier is generated from user-item interaction data and represents various Pareto-optimal trade-offs between the two aspects. Thus, the distance from a point in the frontier provides a joint score of fairness and relevance. As DPFR can be used with existing measures, it is modular and intuitive. Our experiments with 4 recommenders and 3 fairness-aware re-rankers on 6 real-world datasets reveal inconsistent associations between existing measures and DPFR, showing that they are not reliable proxies for evaluations based on Pareto optimality. We further examine the robustness of DPFR as a joint evaluation approach for fairness and relevance by conducting experiments with artificial recommendations and with various fairness-relevance trade-offs, as well as by analysing DPFR's agreement to simple measures of fairness and relevance. These analyses show that DPFR is more robust than existing joint evaluation approaches. Finally, we provide guidelines for evaluating fairness and relevance jointly with DPFR. Our code: https://github.com/theresiavr/robustness-DPFR-recsys-evaluation.
