### [1] [AGR: Agentic Generative Retrieval with Tool-Augmented Search](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26831)
*Ming Yang,Yaqi Yin,Zhibo Wang,Xiaoze Jiang,Jingwei Zhuo,Chao Zhang,Quanzhi Zhu,Weiguo Zheng,Liang Zeng,Yunfeng Zhao,Jiaqiang Liu,Kefeng Wang,Yupeng Huang,Kaiqiao Zhan,Xuanping Li*

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: This paper proposes Agentic Generative Retrieval (AGR), a tool-augmented generative retrieval paradigm that adaptively acquires required features during hierarchical Semantic ID generation to address the inefficiencies of existing generative retrieval methods in exploiting heterogeneous features, and validates its effectiveness through experiments and an online A/B test.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Existing generative retrieval methods cannot efficiently exploit rich heterogeneous features in large-scale search and recommendation systems: static feature injection increases serving cost and introduces irrelevant/conflicting evidence, and existing methods cannot adaptively determine when a feature is needed, which feature to select, or where it should influence hierarchical Semantic ID generation.

Method: Propose AGR, a tool-augmented paradigm that interleaves Semantic ID generation with on-demand feature acquisition: AGR adaptively decides whether to invoke a tool, which feature to acquire, and where the acquired feature influences subsequent decoding based on the request and decoded SID prefix. Construct tool-augmented trajectories based on feature applicability and information gain along the SID hierarchy, and train the model via SID pretraining followed by tool-augmented fine-tuning.

Result: Experiments on an industrial dataset, three public benchmarks, and a real online A/B test all consistently show that AGR outperforms existing generative retrieval methods and improves retrieval effectiveness.

Conclusion: The proposed adaptive agentic generative retrieval paradigm with on-demand tool-augmented feature acquisition effectively solves the shortcomings of existing static generative retrieval methods, delivering consistent performance improvements on both offline datasets and online production tests.

Abstract: Large-scale search and recommendation systems derive much of their retrieval quality from rich, heterogeneous features, yet existing generative retrieval (GR) methods struggle to exploit such feature ecosystems efficiently. Static feature injection either encodes all available signals or places them according to predetermined schemes, increasing serving cost and introducing irrelevant or conflicting evidence. More fundamentally, existing methods cannot adaptively determine when a feature is needed, which feature to select, or where it should influence hierarchical Semantic ID (SID) generation. We propose Agentic Generative Retrieval (AGR), a tool-augmented paradigm that interleaves SID generation with on-demand feature acquisition. Conditioned on the request and decoded SID prefix, AGR decides whether to invoke a tool, which feature to acquire, and where its observation should influence subsequent decoding, thereby steering generation toward feature-consistent SID branches. To supervise this behavior, we construct tool-augmented trajectories based on feature applicability and information gain along the SID hierarchy, and train the model through SID pretraining followed by tool-augmented fine-tuning. Experiments on an industrial dataset and three public benchmarks, together with an online A/B test, consistently demonstrate the effectiveness of AGR.

### [2] [OneChord: Distill What You Rank, Recommend in One Pass](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25805)
*Zesheng Li,Chengchang Pan,Honggang Qi*

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: 本文提出了OneChord推荐框架，遵循“提炼你排序的内容”直接训练原则，实现单遍推理推荐，在多个Amazon数据集上提升了排序性能和推理速度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 语义ID推荐部署中，最终排序依赖编码概率与物品相似度的归一化和相对权重，现有方法无法满足严格推理预算下准确的商品候选排序需求，因此需要更高效直接的训练推理框架。

Method: 提出OneChord框架，采用学习到的目录归一化融合进行下一物品监督、精炼蒸馏和推理；共享并行解码器生成联合语义ID证据和物品匹配分数；训练中固定的去噪器提供合法部分语义ID，学生模型评估这些状态构造精炼融合目标，通过共享难样本上的直接与精炼预测传递精炼能力到单遍推荐中；同时刻画了候选条件诱导的融合权重，给出保留精炼预测器全目录Top-K选择的充分条件。

Result: 在六个Amazon数据集上，OneChord在所有数据集上的Recall@5和NDCG@5均高于对比方法，相比最强基线平均相对提升6.30%和7.12%；全测试生成速度相比最快基线提升1.04-1.18倍。

Conclusion: OneChord将服务端排序分布作为推荐学习和蒸馏的共同目标，在保证推荐性能的同时提升了推理效率，满足紧推理预算下的商品排序需求。

Abstract: Accurate product discovery on the Web requires recommendation models that rank catalog candidates within tight inference budgets. In semantic-ID recommendation, deployment can fuse code probabilities with item similarities, making the final ranking depend on their normalization and relative weights. This motivates a direct training principle: \emph{distill what you rank}. We present OneChord, which uses one learned, catalog-normalized fusion for next-item supervision, refinement distillation, and inference. A shared parallel decoder produces joint SID evidence and item-matching scores. During training, a frozen denoiser supplies legal partial SIDs; the student evaluates these states to construct refined fused targets. Matching direct and refined predictions on shared hard candidates transfers refinement into one-pass recommendation. We characterize the fusion weights induced by candidate conditioning and establish sufficient conditions for preserving the refined predictor's full-catalog Top-$K$ selection. Across six Amazon datasets, OneChord achieves the highest Recall@5 and NDCG@5 among the compared methods on every dataset, with average relative gains of 6.30\% and 7.12\% over the strongest baseline. It also achieves a 1.04--1.18$\times$ full-test generation speedup over the fastest baseline. OneChord makes the served ranking distribution the common object of recommendation learning and distillation.

### [3] [Decision4Rec: A Jev-Inspired Framework of Decision Models on Knowledge and Active Graphs for Multi-Agent Recommender Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26035)

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: 本文提出Decision4Rec，一个基于Jev启发的模块化开放框架，将决策语言模型应用于多智能体推荐系统，结合知识图谱、多智能体候选生成和残差排序，在多个公开推荐数据集上取得了优于基准模型的效果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 决策语言模型（如商业模型Jev）能够为每个候选选项输出概率而非生成文本，但这类模型尚未被有效应用到推荐任务中，需要合适的框架来适配推荐场景的建模需求。

Method: 1. 将推荐场景日志以知识图谱形式组织为短陈述；2. 采用多智能体池生成推荐候选，残差排序器保留Top30候选；3. 决策模型通过标准化问题接口读取候选和知识信息，输出的结果与排序器得分融合；框架支持任意适配该接口的决策模型，实验测试了商业Jev、开源Kev以及作者自行训练的不同规模决策模型。

Conclusion: Decision4Rec框架能有效将决策语言模型应用于推荐，带来显著性能提升，模型增益主要来自多智能体候选池带来的候选信息，更大规模的决策模型能进一步提升性能，开源的中等规模模型已经可以超过商业Jev模型。

Abstract: Decision models, such as the commercial Jev, are language models that return one probability for each option of a question instead of generating text. We present Decision4Rec, an open and modular framework that makes decision models useful for recommendation. A knowledge graph writes the logs of each recommendation scenario as short statements on a defined scale, such as "sold: top 10%". A pool of agents, proposes candidates, and a residual ranker keeps the best 30. A decision model, trained once on real choices of three scenarios, reads them through one interface of typed questions, and a fusion step combines its answers with the score of the ranker. Any model with this interface can be used, and we run the commercial Jev, the released open Kev and Kev models trained by us on recommendation requests. The framework with a trained 4B decision model is 25 to 36% above the best trained baseline in NDCG@10 in three sparse Amazon catalogues, one of them never used to train the decision model, and almost tie with baselines on the dense Steam dataset. Most of the gain comes from the pool, and with a trained recommender it comes from what the agents know about its candidates rather than from new candidates. The 27B decision model adds to the pool 5 to 6% above the 0.8B model in three of four domains. A trained 0.8B open model beats the released Jev in eleven of fifteen settings and is never worse. On MovieLens-1M, a dense domain never seen in training, the gain is 5 to 7%. Code, configurations and trained models are available at https://github.com/marcopoli/decision4rec and https://huggingface.co/m-polignano.

### [5] [Concerns in Benchmarking Jailbreaks: An Empirical Study of Attack Success Rates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26595)
*Leon Tu Nguyen,Rohan Menon,Rafael Tappe Maestro,Eric Aubinais,Chih-Hong Cheng*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 本文对大语言模型越狱攻击基准测试中常用的攻击成功率（ASR）指标进行了实证研究，发现ASR结果受评估流程、评估器类型、尝试次数和排列方式、采样策略等因素显著影响，会导致攻击方法排名反转，还揭示了重复采样会暴露模型残留不安全行为的安全问题，最后针对越狱攻击基准测试提出了明确的实践建议。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当前越狱方法普遍使用攻击成功率（ASR）进行比较，但ASR的计算和比较高度依赖响应生成和判断的流程，不同评估设置下的结果差异未被系统研究，这种不确定性会影响越狱攻击方法比较的公平性和可靠性，还可能掩盖模型的潜在安全问题，因此需要对基准测试中的相关问题进行实证分析。

Method: 选取8种越狱攻击方法，针对3个开放权重大语言模型，采用3种不同的评估流程开展实验；分别测试了不同评估器（关键词匹配、遵循StrongREJECT评分规则的Qwen3评估器）、不同尝试次数与排列方式、不同响应采样策略下ASR结果的变化，分析不同设置对攻击成功率和攻击方法排名的影响。

Abstract: Jailbreak methods are commonly compared using Attack Success Rate (ASR), but these comparisons depend on how responses are generated and judged. In a separate experiment, sampling 100 responses for each unchanged request at Ollama's default temperature of 0.8 yields 100% ASR under keyword matching but 50% under the Qwen3 evaluator. Beyond evaluator sensitivity, these findings raise a safety concern: repeated sampling can expose residual unsafe behavior despite an initial refusal, suggesting probabilistic suppression rather than robust exclusion. We recommend explicit success definitions, matched response budgets, validated evaluators, and reporting cumulative failure under repeated interaction rather than relying on single-generation tests.

### [7] [A Cue's Hidden Price: Auditing Seller Influence on AI Shoppers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25843)
*Felix Buecheler,Gerardo Berbeglia,Mingyu Guo*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 本文针对卖家内容对代表用户购物的大语言模型推荐选择的影响，提出了价格等价（PE）审计方法来衡量影响大小，在大规模多模型实验中验证了该方法比传统选择率审计分辨能力更强，并发布了基准数据集和代码。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 大语言模型越来越多地被用于代表用户购物，卖家控制的内容（如徽章、背书、框架）会影响模型推荐，现有衡量卖家影响的指标选择率存在上限盲区：当线索足够强几乎每次都被选中时，无法区分不同线索的影响强弱，因此需要更准确的衡量方法。

Method: 提出价格等价（PE）审计方法，通过在平衡、位置控制的强制选择实验中，不断提高被线索标记产品的价格，直到该产品仅在一半时间被选中，将该溢价作为线索的价格等价价值，该阈值可以比较不同类型线索的影响。在113472次试验、7个不同系列模型、2个产品类别上开展实验，同时对决策原生模型Jev进行了首次价格等价审计。

Conclusion: 价格等价审计可以解决传统选择率审计的上限盲区，能更准确区分不同卖家线索对AI购物代理的影响强度，发布的包含各模型线索价格特征的基准可为开发者和审计人员提供可复现的衡量方式。

That poses the question at the core of generative engine optimization (GEO): not whether seller content shifts an agent's choice, but how much. The usual gauge of that influence, how often a cue wins the choice, has a blind spot: once a cue is strong enough to be picked almost every time, the choice rate can no longer show how much stronger one cue is than another. In a counterbalanced, position-controlled forced choice, we raise the cued product's price until it is chosen only half the time, and report that premium as the cue's price-equivalent: a formally defined, policy-conditioned parity threshold that makes very different cues comparable. Priced this way, cues that look equally strong at equal price differ by a wide margin, so choice-rate audits substantially under-resolve seller influence. To our knowledge, we also run the first price-equivalent audit of a decision-native model, Jev, which outputs a choice probability directly instead of generating text; strikingly, its profile is unlike any text model we tested, resisting most cues yet remaining susceptible to the strongest social-proof claims. We release the stimuli, logs, and code as a benchmark that reports each model's cue-price profile rather than a single score, giving developers and auditors a common, reproducible way to measure how content moves AI shopping agents.
