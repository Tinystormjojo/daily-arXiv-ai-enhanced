### [1] [GUIDE: Generative Unsupervised Chinese Query Correction via Phonetic and Visual Shared-ID Encoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25343)
*Lei Yang,Binbin Huang,Jiwei Tan,Xuhui Sui,Chang Tu,Yi Wang,Han Li*

Main category: cs.CL

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种面向中文查询纠错任务的无监督生成框架GUIDE，基于混淆再澄清范式，采用音近形近混淆字符共享ID编码和时间衰减加权损失，在公开数据集和真实业务数据集上均超过基线模型，且在线A/B测试验证了其效果提升。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 中文查询纠错对搜索和查询推荐非常重要，有监督方法依赖成本高昂且难以维护的大规模标注纠错对，无法适应查询词汇的快速演变；现有无监督语言模型纠错方法在短查询场景下，无约束生成容易将模糊输入过度纠正为高频短语，造成用户意图偏移，因此需要更好的无监督纠错方案。

Method: 提出GUIDE生成式无监督框架，遵循混淆再澄清范式：将音近或形近可混淆字符编码为共享ID，通过编码器-解码器架构重构原始查询，将纠错约束在合理的混淆邻域内，可从未标记查询流中学习；设计带时间衰减的查询频率加权损失函数，支持模型快速适应不断变化的查询词汇表。

Result: 在公开数据集QSpell 250K和大规模真实业务数据集KwaiSearch上，GUIDE性能 consistently优于强基线模型；在线A/B测试进一步验证了该模型在纠错质量和下游用户 engagement上均有明显提升。

Conclusion: 所提出的生成式无监督中文查询纠错框架GUIDE有效解决了有监督方法标注成本高和现有无监督方法意图偏移的问题，能够适应查询词汇的快速演变，在离线实验和在线测试中都取得了更好的效果，可应用于实际搜索业务。

Abstract: Chinese query correction (CQC) is important for search and query recommendation on content platforms, but supervised methods rely on large annotated correction pairs that are costly to maintain as query vocabularies evolve. Unsupervised correction with language models is attractive, yet in the short-query setting, unconstrained generation often over-corrects ambiguous inputs toward high-frequency phrases, causing intent drift. We propose \textsc{GUIDE}, a generative unsupervised framework for CQC based on a confuse-then-clarify paradigm. \textsc{GUIDE} encodes phonetically or visually confusable characters with shared-IDs and reconstructs the original query with an encoder--decoder architecture, which constrains correction to plausible confusion neighborhoods while learning from unlabeled query streams. A time-decayed, query-frequency-weighted objective further supports adaptation to rapidly changing query vocabularies. Experiments on \textit{QSpell 250K} and a large-scale real-world dataset (\textit{KwaiSearch}) show that \textsc{GUIDE} consistently outperforms strong baselines, while online A/B testing further confirms gains in correction quality and downstream engagement.

### [2] [InsightSR: Refining Symbolic Regression Search Spaces via Parallel Semantic and Structural LLM Guidance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25291)
*Yating Ling,Wenjing Cun,Zhitang Chen*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 本文提出了InsightSR框架，将大语言模型（LLM）作为引导层集成到遗传编程引擎PySR中，通过语义和结构两个互补途径渐进优化符号回归的搜索空间，结合迭代反馈校正，在多个基准测试上显著优于现有符号回归方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统符号回归方法面对物理意义表达式的巨大组合搜索空间存在搜索困难，现有直接依赖LLM生成表达式的方案也存在不足，需要更高效的搜索空间优化方案来提升符号回归的表达式发现能力。

Method: 提出InsightSR框架，在PySR遗传编程引擎外加入LLM引导层，通过两个互补路径优化搜索空间：1）语义种子路径，提出量纲一致的函数骨架；2）结构特征路径，推荐非线性特征变换；通过迭代累积变换，将基于原始变量的深度表达式树搜索转为基于丰富语义特征集的浅树组装；加入生成后反馈回路，根据经验效用分类特征，细化下一轮迭代的引导，将开放式生成转为迭代自校正优化过程。

Result: 在三个基准测试中，InsightSR在Feynman基准上达到95%的精确恢复率，在LLM-SRBench的LSR-Transform任务上达到80.18%的准确率，大幅优于最先进的遗传编程和神经符号方法，同时在真实世界数据集上保持了良好的分布外泛化能力。

Conclusion: 利用LLM从语义和结构两个维度引导优化符号回归搜索空间的InsightSR框架，可以有效缩小搜索难度，显著提升符号回归的表达式发现精度和泛化能力，性能超过现有主流方法。

Abstract: Symbolic regression (SR) seeks to discover parsimonious mathematical laws from observational data, yet conventional approaches often struggle with the vast combinatorial search space of physically meaningful expressions. We present InsightSR, a framework that embeds Large Language Models (LLMs) as a guiding layer around the PySR genetic programming engine. Rather than relying on LLMs to generate expressions directly, InsightSR uses LLMs to progressively transform the search space itself through two complementary pathways: a Semantic Seed Pathway that proposes dimensionally consistent functional skeletons, and a Structural Feature Pathway that recommends nonlinear feature transformations. These transformations accumulate over iterations, broadening the input space and shifting the symbolic search from constructing deep expression trees over raw variables to assembling shallow trees over a rich, semantically informed feature set. A post-generation feedback loop evaluates candidates, categorizes features by their empirical utility, and refines the guidance for the next iteration, transforming the discovery process from open-ended generation into iterative, self-correcting refinement. Across three benchmarks, InsightSR achieves a 95% exact recovery rate on the Feynman benchmark and 80.18% accuracy on the LLM-SRBench LSR-Transform task, substantially outperforming state-of-the-art genetic programming and neural-symbolic methods while maintaining strong out-of-distribution generalization on real-world datasets.

### [3] [MOTIF: Motivation-guided Topology Inference for Cold-start Multimodal Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25381)
*Yurui Shi,Yuchen Miao,Ximing Hu,Zijun Wang,Chang Han*

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: 本文针对冷启动多模态推荐存在的三个问题提出了动机引导拓扑推理框架MOTIF，在三个多模态基准数据集上相比多个基线方法取得了性能提升。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 冷启动多模态推荐面临三个相互关联的挑战：稀疏的交互导致用户意图模糊、冷启动商品在拓扑上孤立、基于相似度的商品图可能引发语义漂移，现有方法无法很好解决这些问题，因此需要提出新的框架应对。

Method: 提出动机引导拓扑推理框架MOTIF，集成语义动机推理、知识增强图重构、加权图对比学习和语义结构对齐四个模块；利用大语言模型离线推理得到动机语义，重构可迁移的商品-商品拓扑，在学习鲁棒图嵌入时不将生成文本注入预测过程。

Result: 在三个多模态基准数据集上的实验表明，MOTIF相比基于图的、多模态的、冷启动的以及大语言模型增强的基线方法均取得 consistent 性能提升，相比最强的最新基线最高获得了6.07%的相对提升。

Conclusion: 所提出的MOTIF框架能够有效解决冷启动多模态推荐的现存挑战，在公开基准数据集上相比多个主流基线方法实现了显著的性能提升。

Abstract: Cold-start multimodal recommendation faces three coupled challenges: (i) sparse interactions obscure user intent, (ii) cold items remain topologically isolated, and (iii) similarity-based item graphs may cause semantic drift. To address these issues, we propose MOTIF, a Motivation-guided Topology Inference framework for cold-start multimodal recommendation. MOTIF integrates Semantic Motivation Reasoning, Knowledge-enhanced Graph Reconstruction, Weighted Graph Contrastive Learning, and Semantic-Structural Alignment. It uses offline LLM reasoning to infer motivation semantics, reconstructs transferable item-item topology, and learns robust graph embeddings without injecting generated text into prediction. Experiments on three multimodal benchmarks show consistent gains over graph-based, multimodal, cold-start, and LLM-enhanced baselines, with up to 6.07% relative improvement over the strongest recent baseline.

</details>
