### [1] [Made in Hungary: Comments on the performance of generative language models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18284)
*Mátyás Osváth,Enikő Héja,Noémi Ligeti-Nagy*

Main category: cs.CL

TL;DR: 本文对匈牙利近期三项开发生成式语言模型的工作进行了方法学评估，指出它们在评估协议、训练流程和能力遗忘评估三方面存在明显缺陷，强调了语言模型开发中严谨实验设计的重要性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 匈牙利之前没有具备对应能力的生成式语言模型，现有以英语为中心的模型对匈牙利语支持能力有限，因此诞生了三项相关开发工作，但这些工作存在未被指出的方法学缺陷，需要进行梳理和指出，以促进领域严谨发展。

Method: 本文通过重新在推荐推理设置下评估对比模型、检查数据污染情况、分析训练流程是否符合最佳实践、测试适配模型在原始基准上的性能下降情况，来检验三项匈牙利生成式语言模型开发工作的方法学可靠性。

Result: 1. 原评估协议可靠性存疑，推荐设置下Qwen3-4B得分高于匈牙利适配版Racka-4B，两项工作存在明显数据污染会偏差结果；2. 三项工作训练流程在语料整理和数据混合上不符合当前最佳实践，且缺乏可控消融无法可靠评估选择；3. 三项工作都未评估遗忘/能力损失，测试显示所有适配模型都存在性能下降，Racka-4B尤其严重。

Conclusion: 现有三项匈牙利生成式语言模型开发工作存在多处方法学局限，考虑到语言模型开发需要巨大计算和经济成本，严谨的实验设计十分重要。

Abstract: In recent years, three initiatives have emerged to develop generative language models in Hungary. The motivation behind them is the same. For Hungarian, no model with the given capability existed, or existing English-centric models offered limited proficiency. A detailed examination of the corresponding studies, however, reveals several methodological limitations. First, the reliability of the evaluation protocols is questionable. Contrary to the findings of Csibi et al. [2026], evaluation under the recommended inference settings shows that Qwen3-4B achieves higher scores than Racka-4B, its Hungarian-adapted version. Data contamination is evident in the work of Yang et al. [2025d] and Szentmihályi et al. [2025], potentially biasing the reported results. Second, the training pipelines fall short of current best practices in corpus curation and data mixture, which risks wasting substantial compute on low-quality data. The lack of controlled ablations prevents reliable assessment of these choices. Third, none of the three papers assessed forgetting or capability loss. Testing the adapted models on a subset of the original benchmarks indicates performance decline in all three cases, especially Racka-4B. These observations emphasize the importance of rigorous experimental design in language model development, given the significant computational and financial costs involved.

### [2] ["If I Had to Buy Just ONE: Galaxy S26 Ultra": Auditing AI-Generated Product Recommendations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18729)
*Lucas G. Uberti-Bona Marin,Thales Bertaglia,Giovanni Astante,Bram Rijsbosch,Gijs van Dijck,Anikó Hannák,Gerasimos Spanakis,Konrad Kollnig*

Main category: cs.CL

TL;DR: This paper audits the bias and consistency of AI-generated commercial product recommendations from popular chatbots (ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Google Search AI Overviews), and finds large variations in personal-style preferences, recommended products and cited sources across repeated queries and between interfaces/APIs, concluding that independent audits need to account for these factors.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Consumers increasingly rely on AI chatbots for product purchase advice, and as AI providers monetize via advertising, concerns about bias and impartiality of this AI-generated advice have emerged, so an independent audit is needed to examine the characteristics of current AI commercial recommendations.

Method: The authors curated a dataset of 2,528 real commercial product advice queries (ConsumerQ), collected 1,536 responses from 5 popular AI services (ChatGPT interface/API, Gemini interface/API, Google Search AI Overviews), and analyzed the frequency of first-person preferences, consistency of recommended products across repeated requests, and overlap of cited sources between different services/versions.

Result: ChatGPT expresses first-person product preference in 79% of recommendation responses, compared to only 7% for Gemini and 2% for Google AI Overviews; recommended products often change across repeated requests. Average domain overlap of cited sources for the same query is only 5.4% between ChatGPT and Gemini interfaces, with 76.7% of comparisons sharing no common domain; APIs have different source exposure and lower overlap compared to their corresponding consumer-facing interfaces.

Conclusion: Neither isolated responses nor API observations can represent the commercial advice that real consumers encounter. Independent audits of AI-mediated commercial advice need to consider repeated responses, consumer-facing interface conditions, and the source layer being observed.

Abstract: Consumers increasingly use AI chatbots for advice on what to buy. With companies like OpenAI and Google monetising their AI through advertising, this raises difficult questions about the bias and impartiality of such advice. In response, we conduct an AI audit of popular chatbots using real commercial-advice queries. First, we curate a dataset of 2,528 real commercial-advice queries (ConsumerQ). Then, we evaluate 1,536 responses to product queries from popular AI chatbots: ChatGPT (chatbot and API), Google Gemini (chatbot and API), and Google Search (AI Overviews). We find that ChatGPT expresses a first-person product preference in 79% of product-recommending responses, compared with 7% for Gemini and 2% for AI Overviews, while the products recommended often change across repeated requests. Displayed sources vary strongly: for the same query, the ChatGPT and Gemini interfaces share only 5.4% of domains on average, with no domain in common in 76.7% of comparisons. APIs provide a different view from their corresponding interfaces, with mean domain overlaps of 12.0% for ChatGPT and 14.8% for Gemini, and also differ in the types and layers of source information they expose. Our findings show that neither isolated responses nor API observations can be assumed to represent the commercial advice consumers encounter. Independent audits of AI-mediated commercial advice should therefore account for repeated responses, consumer-facing conditions, and the source layer being observed.

### [3] [Re2A: Situated Conversational Recommendation via Rubric-based Preference Reasoning and Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18249)
*Dongding Lin,Jian Wang,Xiaoyan Zhao,Wenjie Li*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 本文针对情景化对话推荐（SCR）任务提出了Re2A框架，通过基于评分规则的偏好推理和偏好条件优化对齐，在两个公开SCR数据集上实现了优于现有SOTA方法的效果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 真实推荐场景通常存在于共享物理环境中，对应情景化对话推荐任务要求模型同时对对话历史、共同观察场景和场景内商品属性进行推理；现有方法面临两个挑战：难以在对话过程中准确理解情景化用户偏好，以及难以生成同时满足用户需求和符合场景约束的回复。

Method: 将SCR建模为结构化的先推理再对齐过程：1.提出基于评分规则（rubric-based）的偏好推理，利用自动评分规则引导模型生成显式偏好状态；2.基于得到的偏好状态，提出偏好条件优化，将回复生成对齐到用户偏好满足和场景一致性两个目标。

Result: 在两个公开SCR数据集上的大量实验表明，Re2A持续优于现有最优方法，能够生成更精确、更具上下文感知的对话推荐结果，代码已公开。

Conclusion: 所提出的Re2A框架能够有效解决情景化对话推荐中偏好理解和生成对齐的挑战，性能超过当前SOTA方法。

Abstract: Real-world recommendation scenarios are commonly grounded in shared physical environments during user-recommender interactions. This motivates situated conversational recommendation (SCR), a complex task requiring recommender assistants to jointly reason over dialogue history, co-observed scenes, and in-scene item attributes. However, current approaches struggle with this setting due to two intertwined challenges: accurately understanding situated user preferences throughout the conversation and generating responses that simultaneously satisfy user needs and grounded situations. To this end, we propose Re2A, a framework that formulates SCR as a structured reason-then-align process. We introduce rubric-based preference reasoning, which uses automated rubrics to guide the model toward producing explicit preference states. Based on these states, we propose a preference-conditioned optimization to align response generation with dual objectives: user preference satisfaction and situation consistency. Extensive experiments on two SCR datasets demonstrate that Re2A consistently outperforms state-of-the-art methods, delivering more precise, context-aware conversational recommendations. Our code is available at https://github.com/DongdingLin/Re2A.

### [4] [Securing quantum error correction against misleading advice from AI agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19090)
*A. Barış Özgüler*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 本文研究攻击者如何影响AI顾问来对量子纠错更新进行攻击，提出了利用额外校准测量和不确定性约束来防御这类有害攻击的方法，通过仿真和实验验证了该方法能够在保留有益更新的同时拒绝有害更新。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 目前存在攻击者可能操控AI顾问对量子纠错过程引入有害更新的安全风险，同时被动综合症记录中的歧义阻碍了正确的恢复选择，需要解决这一安全和恢复选择问题，保障量子纠错过程的安全性。

Method: 识别出被动综合症记录中阻碍恢复选择的歧义性，提出利用额外对已知编码校准状态的终端逻辑测量来补充缺失的符号信息；设计了基于校准不确定性和漂移界的验证机制，仅当更新能够证明优于当前恢复方案时才接受更新，不依赖AI顾问的正确性保证；通过仿真攻击测试、奇数距离方形环面码实验和表面码实验对方法进行验证。

Result: 仿真攻击中校准置信检查能够拒绝有害提议同时保留诚实建议下的有益更新；推导了校准年龄的足够限制，匹配仿真中考虑评估时间后，特定信道验证界比通用界保留更多有益更新，同时能够阻止有害激活；表面码实验中确定性控制器在相同观测下实现至少同等数量的有益更新，违反漂移假设时会在环面实验中出现有害接受。

Conclusion: 该方法明确了恢复选择所需的信息，建立了对抗有害更新的条件保证，同时量化了保守接受策略所放弃的恢复改进，能够在防止有害量子纠错更新的同时保留大部分有益更新。

Abstract: Can an attacker turn influence over an artificial intelligence (AI) adviser into a harmful quantum error-correction update? We identify an ambiguity in passive syndrome records that obstructs recovery selection, then show how additional calibration measurements support certified recovery updates under uncertainty and drift. In an odd-distance square toric code with error-free preparation, syndrome measurements, and recovery operations, opposite coherent $X$ rotations produce identical passive syndrome-history distributions. Yet a fixed phase correction can help at one sign and harm at the other. A terminal logical measurement on known encoded calibration states supplies the missing sign information. A separate evaluator accepts an update only when calibration uncertainty and a justified drift bound certify improvement over the current recovery, without assuming that the adviser recommends correctly. In simulated advice attacks, calibration-confidence checks reject harmful proposals while retaining beneficial updates under honest advice. We derive sufficient limits on calibration age that require improvement through deployment. In matched simulations, a validated channel-specific bound retains more beneficial updates than the general bound after accounting for evaluation time, while preventing the tested harmful activations under the stated drift assumption. A separate surface-code experiment includes stochastic circuit faults and noise changing during acquisition. Deterministic controllers achieve at least as many beneficial updates with the same observations. Violating the drift assumption permits harmful acceptance in the toric experiment. The results identify information required for recovery selection, establish conditional guarantees against harmful updates, and quantify the recovery improvements forgone through conservative acceptance.

### [5] [LIGE-GR: A Smooth Leap from Ranking to Generative Recommendation in the LLM Era](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18148)

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: This paper proposes LIGE-GR, a framework that smoothly upgrades existing mature traditional ranking-based recommender systems to generative recommendation by extending them to listwise generation without full rebuilding, and validates it on Meta's short-video platforms achieving positive business gains with limited extra cost.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Large language models (LLMs) have shown great success and offer inspiration for next-generation recommender systems, as both recommendation and language generation target producing ordered sequences for good user experience. However, two key open challenges exist: 1) how to properly integrate LLM-style sequence-level generation and optimization into recommendation; 2) wholesale replacing mature industrial recommendation systems (which have been customized for years around products, constraints, infrastructure and organization) is technically risky and organizationally disruptive, so a smooth transition is needed.

Method: The authors propose LIGE-GR, a listwise generation and evaluation framework that upgrades traditional itemwise ranking recommendation to generative recommendation. Instead of rebuilding the entire recommendation stack from scratch, it generalizes existing pointwise recommendation systems into a listwise generation system, which preserves compatibility with existing models, value functions and serving infrastructure while enabling the system to benefit from listwise optimization from the generative paradigm.

Result: LIGE-GR is validated on real-world short-video recommendation scenarios: Instagram Reels and Facebook Video. It improves user time spent by 1.14% on Instagram Reels and 0.72% on Facebook Video, and only requires modest additional inference resources.

Conclusion: LIGE-GR enables a smooth, low-risk transition from traditional ranking recommendation to generative recommendation for mature industrial systems, compatible with existing infrastructure and delivers measurable business improvements with limited extra inference cost.

Abstract: The remarkable success of large language models (LLMs) has provided important inspiration for the next generation of recommender systems. Structurally, recommendation and language generation share a similarity: both aim to produce an ordered sequence that optimizes the user's experience. However, how to precisely absorb the essence of the LLM paradigm into mature industrial recommender systems remains an open problem.

Replacing such systems wholesale is often technically risky and organizationally disruptive.

Instead of rebuilding the entire recommendation stack from scratch, LIGE-GR generalizes the existing pointwise recommendation system into a listwise generation system. This allows mature recommender systems to benefit from listwise optimization while preserving compatibility with existing models, value functions, and serving infrastructure.
