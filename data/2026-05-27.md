### [1] [ProRL: Effective Reinforcement Learning for Proactive Recommendation via Rectified Policy Gradient Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28293)
*Hongru Hou,Tiehua Mei,Denghui Geng,Jinhui Huang,Ao Xu,Hengrui Chen,Jiaqing Liang,Deqing Yang*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 针对强化学习应用于主动推荐系统(PRS)时策略梯度估计存在偏差和高方差的问题，本文提出ProRL框架，结合分步奖励中心化和位置特定优势估计机制改进梯度估计，在三个真实数据集上显著优于现有先进PRS方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 主动推荐系统需要优化推荐路径来引导用户偏好转向目标物品，强化学习是适合该序列决策任务的框架，但直接将策略梯度应用到PRS中会存在梯度估计缺陷：一是路径级奖励分解为正均值的步级奖励会产生长度依赖偏差，使梯度偏向延长路径而非有效探索；二是用全路径奖励加权每一步忽略了分解结构，导致梯度方差较高，因此需要解决这两个缺陷来提升RL在主动推荐上的效果。

Method: 提出ProRL强化学习框架，包含两种改进机制：1. 分步奖励中心化：减去期望奖励抵消长度依赖偏差，使延长路径的期望梯度信号为零；2. 位置特定优势估计：利用奖励分解结构计算步依赖基线，降低梯度方差，最终得到能够精准优化路径质量的策略梯度。

Result: 在三个真实世界数据集上的实验表明，ProRL相比现有最先进的主动推荐系统取得了显著更优的性能，代码已公开。

Conclusion: ProRL有效解决了RL应用于主动推荐时策略梯度估计的偏差和高方差问题，性能显著超过SOTA主动推荐方法。

Abstract: Proactive Recommender Systems (PRSs) aim to guide user preference shift toward target items by generating paths of intermediate recommendations. Reinforcement learning (RL) provides a principled framework for optimizing such sequential decision tasks, as path rewards can naturally capture both short-term acceptance and long-term guidance effectiveness. However, naively applying policy gradients to PRS results in deficient gradient estimation. We identify two deficiencies: (1) path-level rewards decompose into step-level rewards with positive mean, creating a length-dependent bias that causes gradients to favor path extension over meaningful exploration; (2) weighting each step by the entire path-level reward ignores the decomposition structure, leading to high gradient variance. To rectify these two deficiencies, we propose an effective RL framework ProRL with two novel mechanisms for proactive recommendation. First, Stepwise Reward Centering subtracts expected rewards to neutralize length-dependent bias, ensuring that path extension yields zero expected gradient signal. Second, Position-Specific Advantage Estimation leverages the reward decomposition structure to compute step-dependent baselines, reducing gradient variance. Together, these mechanisms yield policy gradients that precisely target path quality. Our experiments on three real-world datasets demonstrate that ProRL significantly outperforms state-of-the-art PRSs. Our code is available at https://github.com/hongruhou89/ProRL.

### [2] [BiasEdit: A Training-Free Bias-Detect-and-Edit Framework for Learning Fair Visual Classifiers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28450)
*Jungwook Seo,Yoonsik Park,Changmin Lee,Sungyong Baik*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 本文提出了一个无需训练的模块化框架BiasEdit，用于自动检测视觉数据中的偏差属性并通过图像编辑生成去偏差数据集，以学习公平的视觉分类器，在完全偏差的训练数据上也能达到最优的去偏效果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 网络抓取的视觉数据普遍存在虚假相关性和社会偏差，神经网络会学习数据中的偏差，进而加剧网络服务中的不公平，形成恶性循环；现有去偏方法需要已知偏差属性或依赖人工合成混合，缺乏自动无需手动标注的实用方案，且需要处理偏差对齐样本和偏差冲突样本之间的不平衡问题。

Method: BiasEdit是模块化的训练-free框架，分为两步：首先通过对视觉-语言表征进行统计依赖性和互信息分析，自动检测未知偏差属性；然后利用文本引导的图像编辑技术对检测出的偏差属性进行编辑，生成真实的偏差冲突样本，构建去偏差数据集，可直接复用现成的视觉-语言和图像编辑模型，无需手动标注。

Result: 该方法无需手动标注偏差属性，不依赖合成混合，在训练数据完全偏差的情况下，仍然取得了优于现有方法的去偏性能，达到了当前最优水平。

Conclusion: BiasEdit是一种有效自动处理Web来源视觉数据偏差的框架，能够缓解数据集导致的模型偏差，在完全偏差的训练数据上实现了最优的去偏公平分类效果。

Abstract: Visual data from the Web power image classifiers, which often underpin many web services, such as recommendation and content moderation. However, the raw Web data often contain spurious correlations and social biases, and neural networks are known for their tendency to learn biases present in data. This can reinforce unfairness in web services and the web data, leading to a vicious cycle. In the context of image classification, networks learn bias attributes for a specific class when a majority of images contain the same attribute only for a given class. Hence, training a fair and debiased classifier from a biased dataset demands handling an imbalanced problem between a majority of images with bias attributes (bias-aligned samples) and a minority without (bias-conflict samples). In this work, we introduce BiasEdit, a modular framework that automatically detects bias attributes from the original dataset and edits them to construct a debiased dataset. Specifically, BiasEdit first detects unknown bias attributes via statistical dependence and mutual information analysis of visual-linguistic representations, and then explicitly edits those attributes using text-guided image editing to generate realistic bias-conflict samples. Unlike prior works that assume known bias attributes or relies on synthetic mixing, our method operates without manual annotations and can leverage off-the-shelf vision-language and editing models. BiasEdit addresses a fundamental challenge in Web-sourced visual AI, mitigating dataset-induced bias and achieving state-of-the-art debiasing performance even when training data are fully biased.

### [3] [Mixture-of-Experts Knowledge Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-Agent LLM-based Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28175)
*Shijie Wang,Chengyi Liu,Yujuan Ding,Shanru Lin,See-Kiong Ng,Xu Xin,Wenqi Fan*

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: 本文提出了一个名为MixRAGRec的合作多代理知识图谱检索增强生成框架，用于解决基于大语言模型的推荐系统中 outdated知识、检索粒度不匹配、知识结构丢失和端到端训练困难等问题，在真实数据集实验中验证了其有效性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 基于大语言模型的推荐系统通常依赖参数化知识，存在知识过时问题，KG-RAG虽能将推荐基于结构化最新知识，但仍存在三个核心挑战：1. 用户查询复杂度不同，现有方法采用一刀切检索策略，导致简单查询过检索、复杂查询欠检索；2. 将图结构知识转换为线性文本会引入噪声并丢失结构信息；3. 检索粒度选择缺乏直接监督，难以端到端学习查询感知检索。

Method: 提出MixRAGRec合作多代理框架，包含三个模块：1. 混合专家检索代理，将不同查询路由到对应不同粒度的KG检索专家；2. 知识偏好对齐代理，将结构化知识转换为LLM友好的自然语言；3. 对比学习增强的推荐代理，使用对比偏好反馈训练。此外提出混合专家多代理策略优化（MMAPO），在统一目标下联合训练三个代理。

Result: 在多个真实世界数据集上进行大量实验，实验结果证明所提出的MixRAGRec框架比现有方法更有效。

Conclusion: 所提出的多代理框架MixRAGRec以及MMAPO训练方法能够有效解决现有KG-RAG推荐中的挑战，提升基于LLM的推荐效果。

Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) have recently been adopted for recommendations due to their ability to understand user intent and item semantics. However, LLM-based recommender systems often rely on parametric knowledge and suffer from outdated knowledge, motivating knowledge graph retrieval-augmented generation (KG-RAG) to ground recommendations on structured, up-to-date KGs. Despite this promise, effective KG-RAG in recommendations faces great challenges. First, users' queries vary in complexity and require KG knowledge at different granularities, whereas existing methods adopt a one-size-fits-all retrieval strategy, leading to over-retrieval for simple queries and under-retrieval for complex ones. In addition, augmenting LLMs with KG knowledge requires translating graph-structured data into linear text, which may introduce noise and cause structural information loss. Moreover, the selection of retrieval granularity lacks direct supervision and must be inferred from the final recommendation after alignment and downstream utilization, making query-aware retrieval hard to learn end-to-end. To address these issues, we propose MixRAGRec, a cooperative multi-agent framework for KG-RAG recommendations. MixRAGRec integrates a Mixture-of-Experts Retrieval Agent that routes each query to a KG retrieval expert with different granularities, a Knowledge Preference Alignment Agent that converts structured knowledge into LLM-friendly natural language, and a Contrastive Learning-reinforced Recommendation Agent trained with contrastive preference feedback. Notably, we introduce Mixture-of-Experts Multi-Agent Policy Optimization (MMAPO) to train three agents under a unified objective. Extensive experiments on real-world datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of our framework.

### [5] [Can It Reach the Generator? Investigating the Survival of Prompt-Injection Attacks in Realistic RAG Settings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28017)
*Yu Yin,Shuai Wang,Bevan Koopman,Guido Zuccon*

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: This paper re-evaluates prompt-injection generative engine optimization (GEO) attacks in realistic retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) recommendation systems, finding prior work overstates attack success, only LLM-driven attacks remain effective end-to-end, and attacks are easily detectable with a lightweight guard model.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Prior GEO research on prompt-injection attacks for RAG-based recommendation assumed attacked documents always reach the generator by bypassing retrievers and rerankers, which is unrealistic for real deployed RAG systems, where attack modifications impact document retrieval and ranking, so there is a need to evaluate attack effectiveness under realistic full pipelines.

Method: Seven existing GEO attacks are re-evaluated on a realistic three-stage RAG pipeline consisting of retriever → LLM reranker → LLM generator. A lightweight prompt-injection guard finetuned on a small attack dataset is also tested for attack detection.

Result: Prior evaluation protocols substantially overstate attack effectiveness; gradient-based and instruction override attacks mostly fail to reach the generator and collapse in effectiveness, while only LLM-driven prompt injections remain effective end-to-end. The lightweight guard finetuned on small attack data detects all tested attacks.

Conclusion: Previous work overestimates the threat of most GEO prompt-injection attacks in real RAG recommendation systems; only LLM-driven attacks pose a meaningful end-to-end threat, and current attacks are easily detectable with lightweight detection methods.

Abstract: Recent generative engine optimisation (GEO) research has shown that prompt-injection attacks can push a target product to the top of an LLM's recommendation list, with the strongest attacks reporting around $80\%$ success and raising serious security concerns about RAG-based recommendation. However, these results assume the attacked document is always fed directly to the generator, bypassing the retriever and reranker. This is unrealistic: in deployed RAG systems, the attack modifies the document content, which can in turn change whether the document is retrieved and reranked highly enough to reach the generator at all. In this paper, we re-evaluate seven GEO attacks under a realistic three-stage pipeline (retriever\,$\to$\,LLM reranker\,$\to$\,LLM generator). We find that prior protocols substantially overstate attack effectiveness: gradient-based and instruction override attacks largely collapse before reaching the generator, and only LLM-driven prompt injections remain effective end-to-end. Our analysis further reveals that current GEO attacks are easily detectable: a lightweight prompt-injection guard finetuned on a small attack dataset already detects every attack. Our code and data are available at https://anonymous.4open.science/r/geo_injection_rag_survival_anonymizations-8C12.

</details>
