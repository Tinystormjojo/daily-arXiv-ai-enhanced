### [1] [Out-of-Network Attention Dynamics on Bluesky](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01971)
*Andrea Failla,Veronica Mesina,Giulio Rossetti*

Main category: cs.SI

TL;DR: This paper studies out-of-network attention dynamics on the decentralized microblogging platform Bluesky, analyzing large-scale interaction data to quantify how much user attention goes to content from outside existing follow ties, characterize patterns of out-of-network exploration across users and time, and measure its conversion to new follow connections.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: While most personalized social media platforms use explicit follow graphs to curate user content, the scale and characteristics of user attention crossing non-followed network ties are still not well-documented at a large scale, so this work fills this gap by investigating this question on the Bluesky platform.

Method: The authors analyze 173 million user-author interactions (likes, reposts, replies, quotes) from a near-complete Bluesky platform dump collected between February and September 2023. They decompose interactions by attention-path length in the follow graph, compare observed results to an expected-degree null model, and characterize exploration patterns across users and user tenure, and measure conversion rates of out-of-network attention to new follow ties.

Result: Results show 74.5% of interactions are with accounts already followed, and 80.6% of interactions are within two follow hops of the user (much higher than the 22.4% predicted by the null model). Three quarters of all exploratory out-of-network activity is generated by a small broad-reaching minority of users. Aggregate declines in exploration with user tenure hide three distinct individual trajectories. Attention beyond two hops converts to new follow ties at less than one third the rate of two-hop attention.

Conclusion: On Bluesky, out-of-network exploration is substantial in overall volume but is strongly constrained by network proximity, and rarely translates into the formation of new social follow ties.

Abstract: Personalized social media commonly relies on explicit follow graphs to shape what content users encounter; yet how much attention crosses ties they have not formed remains largely undocumented at scale. We study this question on Bluesky, a large decentralized microblogging platform whose default feed relies on a simple, reverse-chronological content recommender. We analyze 173 million user-author interactions (likes, reposts, replies, and quotes) collected from a near-complete platform dump between February and September 2023. We decompose each interaction by attention-path length (already followed, relayed by a followed account, reachable within two follow-hops, or beyond) and find that 74.5% of interactions reach the user through an account they already follow. Measured by distance in the follow graph rather than by route, 80.6% of interaction lands within two follow hops, far beyond the 22.4% an expected-degree null predicts. We then characterize how exploration varies across users and over tenure. A broad-reaching minority generates three quarters of all exploratory activity, while aggregate declines in exploration with tenure mask three distinct individual trajectories. Finally, attention reaching beyond two hops converts into new follow ties at less than one third the rate of two-hop-local exploratory attention. Together, these results depict a platform where out-of-network exploration is substantial in volume but strongly constrained by network proximity and unlikely to translate into new social ties.

### [2] [Cybernetic and Epistemic: A Missing Vocabulary for Trustworthy Agentic Delegation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00961)
*Jérémie Lumbroso*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 本文针对当前AI代码生成委托场景中人类监督缺乏清晰概念区分的问题，提出区分语言在委托中扮演的控制论（协调行动）和认知（协调理解）两种功能，给出可第三方检验的智能体系统治理准则、可操作的重构测试和记录规范，解决可信代理委托中词汇缺失的问题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 随着越来越多代码生成工作委托给AI系统，开发瓶颈从写代码转向监督AI写代码，现有领域谈论"人类监督"时，没有区分语言在委托渠道中的两种不同作用，存在词汇缺口，导致出现认知形式语言承担控制论工作、输出为获得批准而非贴合事实的问题，使得监督流于形式，无法真正问责智能体，因此需要补充缺失的概念框架。

Method: 首先从概念上区分了语言在委托中的两种功能：协调行动的控制论功能和协调理解的认知功能；然后通过三个委托案例（一个实践可行且可审计的认知参与案例、一个按自身条款撤回推荐的公开记录案例、一个不需要给出决策理由的失败监督案例）进行说明；最后提出智能体系统治理准则，给出可操作的两部分重构测试来评估委托记录，以及要求每条记录都包含决策条件的ORRCF商议记录规范。

Result: 清晰区分了控制论与认知两种语言功能，明确了当前监督失效的根源；通过案例展示了可审计的认知参与的可行性，以及流于形式的监督的问题；提出了可操作的治理准则、重构测试方法和ORRCF记录规范，能够让第三方区分有效决策和橡皮图章式的无效审批。

Conclusion: 可信代理委托领域缺少区分语言两种功能的关键词汇，现有监督模式容易流于形式；提出每个重要决策都需要包含第三方可检验的、可改变决策结果的条件这一治理准则，配合重构测试和ORRCF规范，可以实现可问责、可审计的有效监督，解决词汇缺口带来的问题。

Abstract: As code generation is increasingly delegated to AI systems, the bottleneck is shifting from writing code to supervising the systems that write it --- a shift CS-education researchers have begun to name. This shift exposes a vocabulary gap: the field asks for "human oversight" without a working distinction between the two things language does in a delegation channel --- coordinate action (cybernetic: words succeed when the world comes to match them) and coordinate understanding (epistemic: they succeed when they answer to the world and a hearer can check that they do). The failure this names is not cybernetic language but epistemic-form language doing cybernetic work: explanation-shaped output calibrated for approval rather than truth. Oversight that checks only whether an output was approved is satisfiable by rubber-stamping; oversight that holds an agent accountable requires the reasoning behind its work be retrievable and checkable. We present three delegation episodes --- illustrations, not controlled evidence --- in which epistemic engagement proved practicable while remaining auditable, one public record where a recommendation was withdrawn on its own stated terms, and one failure case illustrating oversight that requires no reasons for its discretionary choices. We propose a criterion for agentic-system governance, alongside existing technical trust properties: every consequential choice should carry the condition under which it would have gone otherwise, in a form a third party can test. Without such a condition, a third party cannot distinguish a decision from a rubber stamp. We give the criterion an operational form --- a two-part reconstruction test scoring a delegation record by whether a second reader can predict what the agent does under a perturbation --- and a deliberation-recording convention, ORRCF, that makes the condition a required component of every recorded choice.

### [3] [Neither Black nor White: Balancing Semantic and Collaborative Signals with Graph-Informed Semantic IDs (GrIS)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01533)
*Aleksei Medvedev,Alejandro Ariza-Casabona,Steven Derby,Gonzalo Fiz Pontiveros,Xinyang Shao,Florian Spiess*

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: 本文重新定义了生成推荐中语义ID（SID）的构建问题，将其从表示学习问题重构为递归图聚类问题，提出了统一框架GrIS（Graph-Informed Semantic IDs），给出两种实例化方法，在多个真实数据集上相比现有最优方法取得显著性能提升。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有生成推荐中的语义ID构建方法将其视为表示学习问题，仅依赖内容量化，忽略了结合语义内容信息和协同信号的图结构信息，没有挖掘出更广阔的设计空间。

Method: 1. 将SID构建重构为对同时携带语义内容（节点）和协同信号（边）的图进行递归聚类，将SID分配转化为层次图划分问题，提出统一框架GrIS；2. 指出原有RQ-VAE和RQ-KMeans是图为空时GrIS的特例，开辟了图构造和递归划分算法两个独立可配置的设计维度；3. 给出两种实例化方法：基于可微分图池化实现层次分配的RecDMoN，以及扩展RQ-VAE引入图感知物品表示和图重建目标的RQ-GAE。

Result: 在多个真实世界数据集上，GrIS consistently优于感知协同过滤的现有最优方法，Hit@10最高提升了52%；由于图构造和划分是独立可配置组件，两个维度上的改进可以被组合并系统评估。

Conclusion: 将语义ID构建重构为层次图递归划分问题是有效的，提出的GrIS框架统一并包含了现有方法，能够灵活结合语义和协同信号，显著提升生成推荐性能，且支持对不同组件改进的系统组合与评估。

Abstract: Existing work on Semantic IDs (SIDs) for generative recommendation treats SID construction as a representation learning problem: encode items into a quantised latent space and read off codes. We argue this view is incidental. SID construction is, at heart, a recursive clustering problem, and once stated this way the natural object to cluster is a graph whose nodes carry semantic content and whose edges carry collaborative signal; SID assignment becomes a hierarchical graph partition. This reframing yields a unified framework, Graph-Informed Semantic IDs (GrIS), that subsumes prior approaches rather than displacing them. RQ-VAE and RQ-KMeans are recovered as the special case where the graph is empty, exposing content-only quantisation as one corner of a larger design space along two so-far-collapsed axes: graph construction and recursive partition algorithm. We explore two contrasting instantiations: RecDMoN, which performs hierarchical assignment via differentiable graph pooling, and RQ-GAE, which extends RQ-VAE with graph-aware item representations and a graph reconstruction objective. On multiple real-world datasets, GrIS consistently improves over CF-aware SOTA, with gains of up to +52\% Hit@10. Because graph construction and partition are explicit, separately configurable components, improvements on either axis can be combined and evaluated systematically.

### [4] [Not All Is Lost: Repairing Lossy User Preference States of Personalization Encoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01270)
*Parthiv Chatterjee,Dhiraj Golhar,Ummesalma Diwan,Sourish Dasgupta,Manjunath Joshi,Tanmoy Chakraborty*

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: 本文提出了REPAIR方法，用于修复个性化编码器中有损的用户偏好状态，通过利用编码器缓存的各时间步表示提取校正信息修正偏好状态，在多个推荐和个性化生成任务上取得显著性能提升。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 个性化编码器将用户交互历史压缩为偏好状态后，仅基于该状态的任务头会丢失编码器缓存的单个时间步表示中留存的有用偏好证据，存在可恢复性缺口，现有方法（如仅微调任务头）性能提升有限。

Method: 提出REPAIR方法：1）在紧凑的学习坐标空间中比较缓存表示与当前偏好状态；2）从长历史、近期交互和局部突发交互中解析校正证据；3）选择哪些时间步的哪些模式有贡献，将聚合校正添加到任务头输入的偏好状态中；该方法不需要重新编码历史，可复用现有前向计算得到的表示，只需训练REPAIR模块，保持编码器和任务头冻结。

Result: 在MovieLens、PENS、MIND、Amazon Reviews 2023数据集上，对12个代表性推荐模型，仅训练REPAIR就提升了所有模型的MRR和nDCG@10，提升幅度大于仅微调任务头；例如Mamba4Rec在MovieLens上MRR提升3.96，而仅微调任务头仅提升0.19；在个性化生成任务上，IMPerSumm的加权PerSEval指标最高提升25.23%；排名和时间诊断验证了紧凑的、依赖宿主的校正结构的有效性。

Conclusion: 压缩后的偏好状态校正能有效提升个性化模型性能，REPAIR可以在不微调原有编码器和任务头的情况下显著提升推荐和个性化生成效果，区分了偏好证据的可用性和下游使用。

Abstract: Personalization encoders compress evolving interaction histories into preference states used to rank items or condition text generation. A task head operating only on this state can miss useful evidence that remains in the frozen encoder's cached representations for individual timesteps. We study this recoverability gap and propose REPAIR, which compares cached representations with the current preference state in a compact learned coordinate space. It resolves corrective evidence over extended history, recent interactions, and localized bursts. It then selects which patterns at which timesteps contribute and adds their aggregate correction to the state before the task head. Encoder-host repair reuses representations from the existing forward computation without re-encoding the history. Across MovieLens, PENS, MIND, and Amazon Reviews 2023, training only REPAIR improves MRR and nDCG@10 for all twelve representative recommendation hosts while both encoder and task head remain frozen. Head-only finetuning of the same hosts yields smaller gains. For example, Mamba4Rec on MovieLens gains 3.96 MRR points, compared with 0.19 from head-only finetuning. Rank and temporal diagnostics support a compact, host-dependent corrective structure. In personalized generation, IMPerSumm improves the two reported weighted PerSEval variants, which assess responsiveness to user preference, by up to 25.23%. These results support post-compression state correction and distinguish the availability of preference evidence from its downstream use.
