### [1] [Edit Recommendation: Can LLMs Anticipate Edit Requests from Users, and When?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21155)
*Daisuke Kikuta*

Main category: cs.CL

TL;DR: 该工作定义了编辑推荐新任务，探究大语言模型能否提前预判用户针对LLM生成产物的后续编辑请求，基于真实对话日志构建评测基准EditRecBench，提出决策模型门控机制过滤低置信度预测案例，有效提升了编辑推荐的实际可用效果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM智能体生成内容后，用户往往需要额外提交编辑修改请求来调整生成结果，繁琐的审核反馈流程大幅提升用户使用成本；提前预判用户潜在的编辑需求并主动给出推荐，可以有效降低用户的交互负担，但目前学界尚无针对该预判任务的系统研究和对应的标准化评测基准。

Method: 首先将提前预判用户编辑请求的目标正式定义为编辑推荐任务，基于公开真实对话数据集WildChat-4.8M的会话日志构建专属评测基准EditRecBench；针对部分案例本质上难以预测的现实挑战，引入基于决策模型的门控机制，在编辑推荐结果展示给用户前，提前过滤掉预测正确概率极低的候选案例。

Result: 论文通过基准评测发现真实场景中存在部分本质上无法被提前预判的编辑请求案例，所训练的门控决策模型性能优于现有同类型决策模型，在设定的门控阈值下，最终展示给用户的编辑推荐候选的pass@5指标提升了12~25个百分点。

Conclusion: 大语言模型具备一定程度的提前预判用户后续编辑请求的能力，搭配提出的门控过滤机制可以有效规避难预测的无效推荐场景，大幅提升编辑推荐任务的实际可用性，相关研究代码已完全开源供后续领域研究使用。

Abstract: After a large language model (LLM) agent generates an artifact, users often send edit requests for it. This paper studies whether an LLM can anticipate these requests before they are sent. Such anticipation is useful in practice because it reduces the effort of user review and feedback. We formalize this task as edit recommendation and build EditRecBench, a benchmark for evaluating the performance of LLMs on this task, from real-world conversation logs in WildChat-4.8M. Through benchmarking and failure analysis, we identify a practical challenge that some cases are inherently difficult to predict in real-world settings. To address this, we introduce decision model-based gating to filter out cases where predictions are unlikely to be correct before they are presented to the user. Our trained model outperforms existing decision models and raises pass@5 among shown suggestions by 12--25 points at a gating threshold. Our code is available at https://github.com/ntt-dkiku/llm-edit-recommendation.

### [2] [Learning to Reason with Predictive Gains for Generative Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21441)
*Zhuoyang Li,Teng Shi,Jiakai Tang,Weijie Yu,Guofu Xie,Xiao Zhang,Lei Kou,Yang Yu,Lei Shen,Xin Chen,Bing Wang,Xiaoyi Zeng,Jun Xu*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 本文针对现有推理增强生成推荐方法采用GRPO优化、仅基于最终推荐性能设计规则奖励带来的两个核心缺陷，提出基于预测增益的GainReason训练框架，以单步推理带来的预测效果变化作为细粒度奖励信号优化推理生成，在三个公开基准上相比强基线取得了一致且显著的性能提升。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有推理增强生成推荐方法的优化方案存在两大局限：1）无法度量推理过程内单一步骤的预测贡献，个体推理步骤的价值不能直接反映到优化信号中；2）组内学习信号区分度极弱，同一组下不同推理轨迹往往拿到几乎无差别的奖励，严重限制策略优化的效果，因此亟需设计贡献感知、高区分度的优化信号来充分释放大语言模型的推理能力以提升生成推荐性能。

Method: 提出GainReason训练框架：1）定义预测增益PG，度量单条推理语句引发的目标语义SID对数似然的变化，生成贡献感知、高区分度的奖励；2）从理论层面证明期望PG和推荐排序目标是对齐的；3）设计PGRO优化方法，采用句子级PG奖励优化推理生成，配套定制因果注意力机制实现PG的高效计算；4）增设辅助任务PGRU，自适应强化推理条件下的预测过程，作为PGRO的补充。

Result: 在三个公开基准数据集上开展的大量实验结果显示，GainReason相比所有对比的竞争性基线都取得了一致的性能优势，能够通过引入推理带来大幅的性能提升。

Conclusion: 基于预测增益设计的GainReason框架有效解决了现有推理增强生成推荐优化中推理步骤贡献不可度量、组内奖励区分度不足的核心缺陷，可以显著提升生成推荐对推理能力的利用效果，最终大幅改善推荐性能。

Abstract: Reasoning-enhanced generative recommendation (GR) has attracted increasing attention for leveraging the reasoning capabilities of large language models (LLMs) to improve next-item prediction. However, existing methods primarily optimize reasoning through Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) with rule-based rewards determined by final recommendation performance, which faces two key limitations: (i) unmeasured predictive contributions within reasoning, where the predictive contributions of individual reasoning steps are not directly reflected in optimization signals, and (ii) weakly discriminative learning signals within groups, where different reasoning trajectories within the group often receive indistinguishable rewards, limiting effective policy optimization. In this paper, we propose GainReason, a simple yet effective training framework that optimizes reasoning through Predictive Gain (PG). Specifically, PG measures changes in target Semantic ID (SID) log-likelihood induced by individual reasoning sentences, providing contribution-aware and discriminative rewards. We further theoretically establish the alignment between expected PG and the recommendation ranking objective. Building on this signal, we develop PG-based Reasoning Optimization (PGRO) to optimize reasoning generation with sentence-level rewards, supported by a custom causal attention mechanism for efficient PG computation. Additionally, an auxiliary PG-based Reasoning Utilization (PGRU) task adaptively strengthens reasoning-conditioned prediction to complement PGRO. Extensive experiments on three public benchmarks demonstrate that GainReason consistently outperforms competitive baselines and achieves substantial performance gains from reasoning.
