### [1] [DeepAffinity: Long-Term Aspect Preference Prediction in eCommerce using Small Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02468)
*Yotam Eshel,Guy Hadad,Guy Feigenblat,Yuri M. Brovman,Matt Gearhart,Bracha Shapira*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: This paper proposes DeepAffinity, a method based on Small Language Models (SLMs) with structured prompts and specialized prediction heads, to predict long-term user aspect preferences (brand, size, color, etc.) (called Aspect Affinity) in eCommerce. Experiments show it outperforms standard generative fine-tuning and poorly-performing general-purpose LLMs, and improves recommendation quality on a large multinational eCommerce platform.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Fine-grained personalization for recommendation, search and marketing in eCommerce requires accurate prediction of user long-term preferences for product aspects (aspect affinity), which can capture evolving preferences beyond current sessions, and existing methods (general-purpose LLMs without task-specific tuning, standard generative fine-tuning) do not perform well on this nuanced behavior modeling task.

Method: 1. Frame Aspect Affinity as a temporal prediction task, which forecasts users' future aspect choices from their time-ordered interaction history to capture evolving long-term preferences. 2. Propose DeepAffinity, which leverages Small Language Models (SLMs) with structured prompts and specialized task-specific prediction heads, and fine-tunes the model for the Aspect Affinity prediction task.

Result: DeepAffinity outperforms standard generative fine-tuning methods; general-purpose open-source large language models perform poorly on this task without task-specific tuning, demonstrating their limitations in modeling nuanced user behavior; DeepAffinity improves recommendation quality on a large-scale multinational eCommerce platform.

Conclusion: Task-specific fine-tuned small language models with structured prompts and specialized prediction heads are effective for long-term aspect preference prediction in eCommerce, outperforming standard generative fine-tuning and untuned general-purpose LLMs, and can deliver measurable improvements to recommendation quality on real large-scale eCommerce platforms.

Abstract: We explore predicting eCommerce user preferences for product aspects such as brand, size, and color - a task we define as Aspect Affinity. Solving this task improves customer understanding and enables fine-grained personalization in recommendation, search, and marketing. We frame Aspect Affinity as a temporal prediction task: forecasting a users future aspect choices from their time-ordered interaction history, capturing long-term preferences that evolve beyond the current session. To this end, we propose DeepAffinity, which leverages Small Language Models (SLMs) with structured prompts and specialized prediction heads fine-tuned for this task. We show DeepAffinity outperforms standard generative fine-tuning methods, while general-purpose open-source LLMs perform poorly without task-specific tuning, highlighting their limits in modeling nuanced behavior. Finally, DeepAffinity enhances recommendation quality on a large-scale multinational eCommerce platform.

### [2] [GenCAR: Generative Counterfactual Alignment with Risk-Controlled Selection for Out-of-Distribution Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02162)
*Qianqian Wang,Yunshan Li,Jiawen Zeng,Wenwu Gong,Lili Yang*

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: 本文针对分布偏移下的OOD推荐问题，提出了结合偏好 grounded 反事实监督与校准集合选择的GenCAR方法，解决现有方法未控制代理标签错误发现率（FDR）的问题，理论证明了FDR的控制能力，实验验证了方法在多个基准上提升OOD候选恢复效果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 分布偏移下的OOD推荐需要平衡推荐效用和风险，但现有大多数OOD推荐方法在优化排名或构造反事实候选时，没有对服务集合的代理标签错误发现率（FDR）进行控制，存在风险控制缺失的问题。

Method: 1. 将OOD推荐服务形式化为α-Valid Counterfactual Recommendation（α-VCR）问题，在保留反事实监督学习到的候选支持的同时控制代理标签FDR；2. 提出GenCAR框架，固定稳定偏好表示并对环境因子做干预，通过偏好锚和信任半径过滤对离线大语言模型生成的候选进行 grounded，使用 conformal p-value 结合Benjamini--Hochberg方法进行候选选择。

Result: 1. 理论上给出了条件反事实近似误差的界，证明了在可交换性和正回归依赖下可以实现有限样本、无分布依赖的代理标签FDR控制，在任意依赖下也有Benjamini--Yekutieli保证；2. 大量实验验证GenCAR可以有效控制代理错误发现比例，在多个基准数据集上持续提升OOD候选的恢复效果。

Conclusion: GenCAR可以在OOD推荐任务中有效控制代理标签错误发现率，同时提升OOD候选的恢复性能，理论和实验都验证了方法的有效性。

Abstract: Serving useful recommendations under distribution shift is crucial for balancing utility and risk in out-of-distribution (OOD) recommendation. However, most existing OOD methods improve ranking or construct counterfactual candidates without controlling the proxy-label false discovery rate (FDR) of the served set. In this work, we formulate OOD serving as the $α$-Valid Counterfactual Recommendation ($α$-VCR) problem to retain candidate support learned from counterfactual supervision while controlling proxy-label FDR, and propose GenCAR, which couples preference-grounded counterfactual supervision with calibrated set selection. In particular, GenCAR fixes the stable-preference representation while intervening on the environmental factor, grounds offline large language model proposals through preference anchors and trust-radius filtering, and uses conformal $p$-values for Benjamini--Hochberg selection. We theoretically bound conditional counterfactual approximation error and prove finite-sample, distribution-free control of proxy-label FDR under exchangeability and positive regression dependence, with a Benjamini--Yekutieli guarantee under arbitrary dependence. Extensive experiments audit realized proxy false discovery proportions and demonstrate that GenCAR consistently enhances OOD candidate recovery across diverse benchmarks.
